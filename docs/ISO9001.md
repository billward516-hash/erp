# ISO 9001:2015: where the platform provides the evidence

Certification is earned by an organization, not by software. A company running this platform still has to define its quality policy and objectives, decide its processes, and run internal audits and management reviews. What the platform does is make the required **documented information** exist, stay controlled, and be easy to show an auditor. This page maps each clause to the screens and records that support it, and says what remains the company's job.

Founder decision (2026-10-23): management of change must meet ISO 9001 (D-024, D-158).

**Legend:** ✅ supported with retained records · 🟡 partly supported (the company completes it) · ⬜ the company's own (outside the software)

## 6 Planning

| Clause | Support | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 6.1 Risks and opportunities | 🟡 | FMEA with action priority (D-075 to D-078), continuity plans, change screening with risk level, improvement tracking. A QMS-level risk register is the company's document |
| 6.2 Quality objectives | 🟡 | Reports with KPIs (quality, delivery, maintenance, supplier performance). The objectives themselves are set by the company |
| 6.3 Planning of changes | ✅ | **Management of change (D-158).** Before review, each change records its purpose and justification and four things: potential consequences (6.3 a), what it changes in the QMS (6.3 b), the resources it needs (6.3 c), and responsibilities and authorities (6.3 d). The change cannot be submitted without them |

## 8 Operation

| Clause | Support | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 8.1 Operational planning and control | ✅ | Production orders on released revisions, routings, PM. **Unintended and emergency changes (D-158):** recorded as such, with a generated action to review their consequences and mitigate adverse effects before close-out; counted in the change register for management review |
| 8.2.2–8.2.3 Requirements for products | ✅ | Quotes, sales orders and customer requirements on orders; credit and pricing approvals |
| 8.2.4 Changes to requirements | ✅ | **Change management (D-158):** customer impact recorded (none, must be told, must approve) with the customers named; a customer action is generated before startup and must be completed with who and when |
| 8.3 Design and development | 🟡 | Product revisions hold the BOM and routing; released revisions are frozen and changed through new revisions; design FMEA. 8.3.2–8.3.5 design planning, inputs, reviews and verification are the company's process, held as controlled documents and change records |
| 8.3.6 Design changes | ✅ | Change management on revisions: identified, reviewed, authorized, with actions to prevent adverse impact (D-158) |
| 8.4 Externally provided processes | ✅ | Approved suppliers, purchase approvals, receiving inspection, supplier performance scorecards, supplier corrective action requests, re-evaluation every 12 months (D-154) |
| 8.5.1 Controlled conditions | ✅ | Work instructions at the work order and the shop floor, qualified operators, calibrated gauges, SPC with the rules each company sets (D-156) |
| 8.5.2 Identification and traceability | ✅ | Lots and serials with backward and forward trace and recall lists (D-147, D-148) |
| 8.5.3 Customer property | 🟡 | Customer-supplied items can be held as parts and lots; the company's procedure |
| 8.5.4 Preservation | 🟡 | Shelf life and expiry, holds, storerooms; packaging is the company's procedure |
| 8.5.5 Post-delivery activities | 🟡 | Customer returns on credit memos with lots; customer complaints raised as nonconformances |
| 8.5.6 Control of changes | ✅ | **Change management (D-158):** changes are reviewed and authorized through approvals. Every reviewer must record the result of the review in the approval: what was checked and any conditions. The person authorizing is recorded, along with the actions arising, before startup and before close-out. A change revised after approval loses its approvals |
| 8.6 Release of products | ✅ | Pre-startup review on changes; inspection steps; holds on lots until released |
| 8.7 Nonconforming outputs | ✅ | Nonconformances with containment, disposition (concessions need a justification and a second person), lot holds, cost |

## What the company still owns

- The scope, context, quality policy text, objectives and process map, as controlled documents.
- Internal audits as a programme, and the management review meeting and its minutes.
- Deciding which changes go through change management (the screening questions help) and actually raising them.
- Certification with an accredited body. The platform's records are the evidence; they are not the certificate.
