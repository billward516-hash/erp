The implementation planner turns “set the system up” into a list of steps with an owner, an order, a check and a progress bar. It is built around the data imports: loading a file moves its step on by itself, and a person then confirms the data is right. There are three parts: the fit interview (what should we switch on?), the plan (what do we load, and who does it?) and the practice copy (let us rehearse without touching real data).

Administrators build the plan, assign owners, skip steps and unlock steps early. A person who has been made the owner of a step can start and verify that step, and sees Implementation in the menu while they have open steps. Everyone else cannot see the planner.

The fit interview

Open Admin → Fit interview. It asks plain questions: what the company does (makes products, maintains its own equipment, services other people's equipment, sells, runs restaurants or stores, rents property, office only), how many sites and people, whether it keeps stock, buys with purchase orders, sends quotes, must prove training or certifications, records time and expenses, needs the books kept in the system, and what it uses today. Questions that do not apply are not asked.

  1. Answer what you can. A partial set of answers is fine; you can come back.
  2. Choose Save answers and show the recommendation.
  3. Read What we suggest: the recommended Implementation plan depth, the features to Show (each with a reason), those Worth considering, those Probably not needed yet, and a frank list under Not a fit yet (for example: no point-of-sale; scanning needs a connection; no payroll calculation or tax filing in the books).
  4. Optionally choose Hide the features we did not recommend. This sets those features to Nobody (hidden) on the Features page and tells you how many were hidden. Features marked as shown or worth considering stay, and field service is never hidden by this button. You can undo any of it from Features.

You can retake the interview at any time; the recommendation is recomputed from the saved answers. It is a starting point, not a rule: the system never changes anything without your pressing the button.

The implementation plan

Build it

  1. Open Admin → Implementation. If no plan exists you see three choices of depth.
  2. Choose Guided (the essentials: sites, people, customers, sign-in and cutover; for small companies), Standard (adds equipment, parts, storerooms, suppliers, production, maintenance plans, finance and connections; the usual plan) or Full (everything, including history, price lists and open orders).
  3. Optionally set a Target go-live date, then choose Build the plan.

You can change the depth later with the Guidance selector. A deeper depth adds steps; a shallower one removes only steps nobody has touched (no status, owner or note).

The three tracks

  • Data to load: one step for each kind of data (sites, departments, employees, people who sign in, equipment, parts, storerooms, stock, suppliers, customers, documents, bills of material, routings, maintenance plans, chart of accounts, opening balances, open invoices and bills, history and so on). Each step names an import target.
  • Connections: bank statement files, payroll export file, accounting (built-in books or file export), sign-in with your identity provider, and outgoing email. Live bank feeds and direct payroll or accounting connections do not exist; the steps say so.
  • Rehearse and go live: Rehearsal in a practice copy (optional), Final changes and cutover, and Go-live sign-off.

What each step shows

Every step has a title, an owner role (Administrator, HR, IT, Maintenance, Storeroom, Purchasing, Sales, Finance, Payroll, Production, Quality or Safety), a plain description, and a fold-out What to bring and how you know it is right that says what file to ask for (Bring) and the check that proves it is correct (Done when, such as “the count matches the active headcount HR reports”). Data steps have Open the import and template buttons (Template (Excel), Template (CSV)), which give the owner a file with the right column headings, an example row and a data dictionary.

Status of a step

StatusMeaning
To doNot started.
In progressSomeone pressed Start.
Loaded, check itA file for this step's import has been saved. This status is set by the system from the actual imports; nobody types it. The step shows how many records were loaded.
VerifiedThe owner confirmed the data against the “done when” check, with a note saying how.
SkippedAn administrator decided the step is not needed and gave a reason.
LockedA step it depends on is not yet loaded, verified or skipped. The step lists what it waits for.

Work through the steps

  1. Assign an Owner to each step. The person is told, and sees the step in their list.
  2. The owner presses Open the import, loads the file (see Importing data) and comes back. The step now says Loaded, check it.
  3. The owner checks the data against Done when (a count, a spot check, a report that matches), types how they checked into the note, and presses Verify. A data step cannot be verified before something has been loaded.
  4. Reopen with Reopen if a late change is needed.

To start a locked step early, an administrator types a reason (at least 5 characters) and presses Start anyway; the reason is shown on the step and recorded. To skip, press Skip and give a reason.

Readiness and go-live

The tiles Ready, Verified, Loaded, to check and Target go-live summarise progress, and the chips under them show how many steps each owner still has. Only required steps (not marked optional) count. Go-live sign-off can be verified only when every required step is verified or skipped; doing so records the go-live date, and the plan then says “Live since …”. It cannot be skipped.

Practice copy

In the Rehearse and go live section, administrators see a Practice copy card. It makes a separate company where you can rehearse every load, see the problem reports and agree the order, with no risk to the real data. Nothing done there can change the real company.

  1. Choose Make a blank copy with our sites (it starts from your active sites and nothing else; load your sites first) or Make a copy with sample data (two sample plants with employees, equipment, parts, suppliers and customers already loaded).
  2. Choose Open the practice copy. You are signed in to it as an administrator, using a sign-in token that is shown only once, at this moment.
  3. Rehearse. A banner at the top offers Back to the real company.
  4. When done, choose Delete the practice copy and confirm. It is removed completely.

There is one practice copy at a time, and its token is shown only when it is made. If you lose the token (closed the window before opening it), you cannot reopen that copy: delete it and make a new one.

Common mistakes

  • Marking a step Verify with a note like “ok”. The note is the record of how it was checked; say what you counted or compared.
  • Skipping the sites and employees steps and loading history first; the imports will report every row as pointing at something not loaded.
  • Starting a locked step early without a plan to come back and finish its dependency.
  • Forgetting to load the final counts of stock, open invoices and open orders on the cutover day. The re-imports update records by number, so reloading is safe.
