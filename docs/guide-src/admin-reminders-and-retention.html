Two pages under Admin shape the system's daily housekeeping. Reminders decide what the system nags people about, how early, and how often. Data retention decides how long throw-away working data (read notifications, old import rows, sent emails and similar) is kept. Both are applied by the daily job (Scheduled jobs), so changes take effect from the next daily run.

Reminders is open to administrators, and also to anyone an administrator has allowed to change a particular reminder (by default, the managers of that area). Data retention is for administrators only.

Email delivery. Reminders always arrive in the notification bell. The daily email digest is built and queued, and it is sent when the server has a mail server set up (SMTP_HOST, see Configuration); without one the server only writes the messages as files under its storage folder (mail) and sends nothing. The sending code has been verified only against a stand-in mail server in the test suite, not against a real provider (an owner decision, see What is not done), so until you have used Send a test email to me on the Scheduled jobs page and seen it arrive, tell people to rely on the bell. The plan step Outgoing email tracks this.

Reminders

Open Admin → Reminders. The page lists all 38 reminders in groups (Maintenance, Quality, Safety, People, Purchasing and finance, Stock and sites). The line at the top says how many exist and how many are turned off. For each reminder you see what triggers it, who receives it, and whether it is on.

Change a reminder

  1. Find the reminder, for example Calibration due soon.
  2. Tick or untick Send this reminder.
  3. If it has a lead time, set the number of days. The wording follows the reminder: Warn N days ahead; After N days overdue; Every N days; or Flag nothing measured in N days. The allowed range and the default appear under the row; a number outside the range cannot be saved.
  4. Choose how often it repeats: Every day, Once a week or Once only. Each reminder allows only some of these.
  5. Choose Save. The row records who changed it and when.

Rules to know

  • A reminder whose feature is turned off for everyone (Features) is not sent; the row shows a note saying so.
  • Recipients are fixed by the reminder (for example “the assigned technicians” or “quality staff at the site”). You choose whether and how early, not who.
  • Each person can also mute kinds of notification for themselves (Notifications and reminders).
  • Items marked New are reminders for which nothing existed before; look through them when you first open the page.

Who may change a reminder

Administrators see a selector under each reminder: Changed by administrators and managers of the area (the default: whoever holds the area's manage permission, for example quality.manage for calibration) or Changed by administrators and chosen roles, where you tick the roles. Choose Save who may change it. People who may change only some reminders see only those on this page, and the menu entry appears for them.

Periodic reviews

At the top of the Reminders page is a block titled Periodic reviews for records that must be re-confirmed regularly: FMEAs, SPC characteristics and limits, controlled documents, policies and handbook, the expense policy, training course content, user access, approved suppliers, PM plans, critical spares lists, repair templates, store walk checklists and business continuity plans. For each you set whether reviews are tracked, how many months between them, how many days ahead to warn, the repeat, and how many days overdue before managers (or administrators) are told too. Defaults are in the 6 to 24 month range; the page shows the limits. The User access review reminds administrators every six months by default to look at who holds which role; it is a good habit.

Data retention

Open Admin → Data retention. The daily job deletes only short-lived working data, after a period you can adjust. The sentence at the top says how many items the next sweep will remove.

One more period is fixed and cannot be changed: saved replies for retried requests, kept 7 days (they let a phone that lost its connection resend a change without doing it twice).

Change a period

  1. Find the category and type the number of days next to Keep for.
  2. Choose Save. A number outside the allowed range is refused. Each row says how many items are due for removal now and who last changed it.

What is never removed

The page lists it in a card called Never removed: the audit log; business records (assets, work orders, orders, invoices, bills, timesheets, training, quality and safety records); documents and every version; the stock ledger and lot history; accounting sync history; and import summaries. A section Recent sweeps shows what the last five sweeps removed.

Shortening the period for finished import rows reduces how much personal data from old spreadsheets lingers in the system. Once the rows are removed, the problems file for that import can no longer be downloaded (error 410, “the rows of this import were removed …”).
