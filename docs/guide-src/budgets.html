Budgets work in two directions at once. Bottom-up: each site and department builds its own lines by spend category. Top-down: finance sets targets, such as a company-wide ceiling, and compares them to the total. When the two agree, finance approves the budget, and from then on you can track actual spend against it. Open Finance → Budgets.

Budgets are part of the Standard edition; the Lite edition does not include them.

Who can do this:

  • See spend history and budgets: maintenance, plant, quality, property, sales and HR managers, supervisors, buyers, executives, finance approvers (permission "view budgets").
  • Edit and submit lines: the head of a department can edit and submit that department's lines while the budget is a draft. Finance can edit all lines.
  • Create budgets, set targets, review submissions, approve, close, manage spend categories: finance approvers (permission "manage budgets").

Spend history

The first tab, Spend history, answers "where does the money go?". The headline says how much was spent in the last twelve months and which category took the largest share. Pick a site or leave All sites.

The table has one row per spend category: a small bar for each month, the 12-month total, its share, the average per month, a Run rate (the last three months times four), and 2nd half vs 1st, which shows whether spending is rising or falling. The From column shows how much came from supplier invoices, stock used, expense reports and imported history. Below it, By site and department splits the total.

History counts supplier invoices (not stock purchases), stock used, expense reports and any history your administrator imported. If a warning says some spend is on accounts no category includes, a finance approver should add those accounts to a category.

Spend categories

Finance sees a third tab, Spend categories. Each category collects spend from a list of accounts. An account belongs to only one category so nothing is counted twice. To change a category's accounts, click Edit beside it, change the comma-separated list and click Save.

Create next year's budget

  1. On the Budgets tab, under New budget, enter the Fiscal year. The name is suggested, such as "2027 operating budget".
  2. Pick the month the year Starts in.
  3. Click Create. The budget opens as a draft.

The list shows each budget's status, bottom-up total, company target and approval.

Budget statusMeaning
DraftBeing built. Lines, targets and reviews can change.
ApprovedFinance approved it. It is now the plan to compare actual spend against.
ClosedFinance closed it, usually at the end of the year.

Build the bottom-up lines

Open the budget and stay on the Bottom-up lines tab. Lines are grouped by site and department. Each line has twelve monthly amounts, a year total and a basis that says how it was made.

Start from history

  1. Under Start from history, choose a Site and, if you want, a Department (or leave Whole site (no department)).
  2. Set Change % (it starts at 3) to raise or lower last year's pattern.
  3. Click Propose lines. The system adds a line for every category the site spent on in the last twelve months, keeping its monthly pattern.

Add or adjust a line

  1. To add one by hand, use Add a line: choose site, department and category, enter the Year $ and choose Spread like history or Spread evenly. Click Add line.
  2. To change months, click Edit on the line, change the monthly numbers and click Save.

Submit for review

When a site or department is done, click Submit for review on its block. Submissions have their own status.

Submission statusMeaning
DraftNot yet sent.
SubmittedWaiting for finance's review. Editing is locked unless you are finance.
AcceptedFinance is satisfied.
ReturnedFinance sent it back with a note. Fix it and submit again.

Top-down targets

Finance uses the Top-down targets tab to compare the bottom-up total against a ceiling or goal.

  1. Choose what the target covers: a site, a department and a category, or leave any of them on All. A target with none selected covers everything.
  2. Enter the amount and an optional note such as "board ceiling". Click Set target.
  3. Read the table: Target, Bottom-up and the Gap (bottom-up minus target). A target is Within 2% when they are close, otherwise Bottom-up over or Bottom-up under.
  4. To close the gap by decree, click Allocate by bottom-up or by history. The target is spread over the lines it covers in proportion to those amounts, and they add up exactly.

Reviews and approval

  1. On the Reviews tab, find submissions with status Submitted.
  2. Write a Note if needed, then click Accept or Return.
  3. When everything is ready, click Approve budget at the top of the budget.
  4. At the end of the year, click Close.

The tiles at the top show the bottom-up total, the company target with the gap, the number of lines and the number of submissions waiting for review.

Against actual

The Against actual tab compares the plan with what has been spent. The headline says how much has been spent against the budget for the months elapsed, and a forecast for the year. The forecast is actual to date plus the budget for the months still to come. Committed counts open purchase orders for non-stock items.

StatusMeaning
On trackSpending and the forecast are within budget.
Heading overThe forecast for the year is more than 2% above the budget, or the recent run rate points that way.
OverActual spend plus committed orders is more than 5% above the budget to date.
No budgetMoney is being spent in a category and site with no budget line.

The Budget against actual report in Reports shows the same figures and can be exported.

Common mistakes

  • Editing after submitting. Once submitted, a department cannot edit unless finance returns it.
  • Leaving accounts out of categories. Spend on an uncategorized account never reaches history or the budget comparison.
  • Treating a target as a limit on spending. It only compares totals. It does not stop purchases.

History can be filled with last year's spend through Data import → Spend history. A reminder goes to finance when next year's budget has not been started. See Importing data, Reminders and retention and Sites and people (a department's head is named there).
