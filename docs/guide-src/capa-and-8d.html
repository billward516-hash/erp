CAPA means corrective and preventive action. A corrective action deals with a problem that has happened: it finds the root cause, fixes it, and later checks that the fix held. A preventive action deals with a problem that has not happened yet but could. An 8D is a corrective action run in an eight-discipline format, which customers often require after a complaint. All three use the same screens.

Who can do this:

  • See the CAPA page and list: quality reviewers, quality managers and other staff with "view quality".
  • Open a CAPA, change its owner, record the effectiveness check: quality reviewers and quality managers (permission "manage quality").
  • Edit a CAPA and add actions: its owner, or a quality manager. For a CAPA that came from a safety incident, a safety manager can also edit it.
  • Do an action assigned to you: the action's owner.

The CAPA page

Open Quality & safety → CAPA & 8D. A sentence at the top gives the headline. The tiles show open CAPAs (split into corrective and preventive), overdue ones, how many wait for an effectiveness check, the share of fixes that held over the last 12 months, the average days to close, and how many 8Ds are in progress. Tables show open CAPAs by step, by age and by source, and Repeat problems: any part or supplier that needed more than one corrective action this year. Below, the Open list can be filtered to corrective only or preventive only. Overdue ones are marked in red.

Open a CAPA

A quality manager clicks Open a CAPA. CAPAs also start from other pages: a nonconformance (Open a corrective action), a safety incident, an audit finding, an SPC signal, an FMEA row, a change that did not achieve its purpose, and a supplier problem. Those arrive already linked to where they came from.

  1. Choose Corrective (a problem that happened) or Preventive (a problem that could happen).
  2. Choose the site and the source: internal finding, customer complaint, audit, supplier, SPC alarm, change, FMEA, management review, or risk or trend. Preventive actions can't use customer complaint or supplier as the source.
  3. Choose the owner (someone with access to the site), the priority and the due date (the default is 30 days).
  4. Write a title and the problem statement: what, where, when, how many.
  5. For a corrective action, tick Run as an 8D if the customer asked for one.
  6. Click Open corrective action (or preventive action).

The steps and what each needs

The CAPA page shows a progress bar with these steps. The owner or a quality manager moves it forward with the button at the bottom. Each step checks its evidence first.

Root causeFind why it happened.State the root cause and how it was found (5 whys, fishbone, 8D or other).
ActionsDo the work that removes the cause.Finish or cancel every open action, with at least one done.

ClosedThe check showed the fix worked.—
CancelledDropped.—
  1. Open the CAPA and fill Containment and Root cause as you learn them. Click Save when the button appears.
  2. Under Actions type the New action, choose an owner and due date, and click Add action. Each owner gets it on My actions (see Quality).
  3. Owners type what was done and click Done. A note of at least three characters is needed.
  4. Attach evidence and files at the bottom of the page.
  5. Click Move to … at each step.

A CAPA from a supplier problem also has a Supplier section where you record the supplier's answer (their root cause and containment can be copied across). It shows whether the answer is waiting, answered, or past due.

The effectiveness check

When you move to the effectiveness check, a date is set 30 days ahead unless one was already set. The check is done by quality, not by the owner, and not before that date. The owner can lengthen the wait but not shorten it.

  1. When the date arrives, a quality manager who is not the owner opens the CAPA.
  2. Choose Effective: close it or Not effective: more actions needed.
  3. Under Evidence, enter the data, audits or samples that show it (at least ten characters).
  4. Click Record check.

"Effective" closes the CAPA, and nonconformances waiting on it can then close. "Not effective" sends it back to the Actions step.

Running an 8D

An 8D is chosen when the CAPA is opened, or by clicking Run as an 8D while it is still at the Containment step. It adds an 8D disciplines checklist that shows what each of the eight still needs. The server checks the same list before each step.

DisciplineWhat it needsNeeded before you leave
D1 TeamA champion (someone who can give the team time and resources, other than the owner) and at least one team member besides the leader and champion. The owner is the team leader.Containment
D2 Problem descriptionThe problem statement and an is / is not table: what it is, where, and at least one thing it is not.Containment
D3 Interim containmentThe containment, and how you know it works.Containment
D4 Root causesWhy it happened, how it was found, where it should have been caught (escape point), why it was not, and how the causes were confirmed.Root cause
D5 Permanent corrective actionsAt least one action marked "Corrective (D5)".Actions
D6 Implemented and validatedThe corrective actions done, and the effectiveness check.Closed by the check
D7 Prevent recurrenceSystemic changes recorded (or why none are needed), and every read-across decided. A read-across lists where else the same cause could act; for each, say whether it applies and what was found or done.Actions
D8 Team recognizedAfter closure, record what the team achieved. The team is told.After closing

For an 8D, each action is marked containment (D3), corrective (D5) or preventive (D7). Click 8D report to open the customer report as a PDF.

Preventive actions

A preventive action uses the same steps but is described as a potential problem and why it could happen. It cannot be run as an 8D.

Common mistakes

  • Stopping at the symptom. "Operator error" is rarely a root cause. Ask why it was possible.
  • Making the owner verify. The system will refuse. Ask another quality manager.
  • Setting a very short wait. A fix needs time to show it holds; the system won't accept the check before its date.
  • Changing the owner during the check. It is blocked. Send the CAPA back to Actions first.

CAPA can be switched off or limited to roles under features. Overdue CAPA and action reminders come from the reminders catalogue. See Features and editions and Reminders and retention.
