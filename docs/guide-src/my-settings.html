People read screens differently. The page My day → How I like to work lets you change how the platform looks for you: how much space there is, how big the text is, how strong the contrast is, and whether reports open as charts or tables. Nobody else's screens change, and your choices follow you to any device you sign in on. This page also explains the fit interview and the occasional “worth a look” suggestion that administrators may see.

Your display settings

Every setting takes effect the moment you choose it. There is no Save button.

  1. Open My day → How I like to work.
  2. Choose an option in any group. The screens change at once.
  3. If you do not like the result, choose the previous option again.

If your browser or computer already magnifies text, try Normal here first. Combining both can make pages very large.

If the page shows an error in red under the options, the choice could not be saved (for example, you have lost your connection). Your screens may look changed until you reload; choose the option again once you are back online.

Worth a look: gentle suggestions

Administrators sometimes see a small card on Home called Worth a look. It says what the system noticed in the company's own records (for example, parts being issued often with no reorder level set) and what a feature could do about it. It is never a pop-up, never blocks anything, never shows a price, and appears at most once a week.

  • Show me takes you to the feature.
  • Not now dismisses it; the same suggestion is not shown again for a while.
  • Stop these suggestions opens this page, where administrators have an extra group, Suggestions, with a tick box: “Show me a ‘worth a look’ idea now and then (at most one a week)”. Untick it to stop them for you.

Only administrators are shown suggestions, and the suggestions card does not appear in the Lite edition. If you are not an administrator you will not see the group on this page.

The company can also switch suggestions off for everyone. They are based only on counts of the company's own records; nothing leaves the system.

The fit interview

The fit interview is for the person setting the system up, not for everyday users. It lives under Admin → Fit interview, so you will see it in the menu only if you are an administrator. You answer plain questions about the business: what the company makes or does, how many sites and people, whether it keeps stock, buys from suppliers, sends quotes, services customers' equipment, rents space, keeps timesheets and expense claims, needs the system to keep the books, and what customers or regulators require it to prove. Questions that do not apply to your business are skipped.

  1. Open Admin → Fit interview and answer the questions. You can retake it any time.
  2. Choose Save answers and show the recommendation.
  3. Read What we suggest: how deep an implementation plan to follow, which features to show (each with the reason), which are worth considering, which are probably not needed yet, and anything that is not a fit yet.
  4. Optionally, choose Hide the features we did not recommend. This is reversible from the Features page.

Hiding a feature removes it from everyone's menu, so check the list before you do it. If you are wondering why your colleagues are missing a menu entry, this and the Features page are the usual causes. See Why some menu items are missing.

The interview, features and the implementation plan work together. See Administrator brief: your first week, features and editions and the implementation planner.

Common mistakes

  • Expecting your settings to change a colleague's screen. They are personal.
  • Setting text to Larger and compact spacing together. It can crowd tables; try Roomy.
  • Looking for these under your browser. They are held by the platform, so they follow your account, not one browser.
