Plants, basements and rural sites often have poor wifi. The platform is built so that a technician's day does not stop when the connection does. This page explains what keeps working offline, what does not, how to use the outbox, and how to put the app on a tablet or phone.

What works offline

Offline support covers the maintenance technician's core routine on work orders and the field technician's day on My day. Two things make it work:

  • Saved copies. While you are online, the app keeps a copy on your device of your My work orders list and of each work order you open. When the connection drops, you still see those, marked “Copy saved on this device [time]: no connection right now.”
  • The outbox. Three kinds of change can be made without a connection: labor hours, parts used, and status changes (Start work, Put on hold, Mark complete). Each is kept on the device, in the order you made it, until it can be sent. On My day the changes are starting a visit, saving the service report draft and submitting the service report (labor, parts, notes, readings, recommended repairs and the customer's signature).

Offline use is specific to work orders and My day. Do not rely on it for other screens. Open My day (and the work orders you need) once with a connection before you go where the signal is weak: that is when the copies are saved.

My day offline

  1. Open Field service → My day while you are online. The tablet saves the day, each visit's details and report draft, and the parts list.
  2. With no connection the page shows Offline and Copy saved on this device. Tap Start the visit, fill in the report and tap Submit report. The stop shows Waiting to send, and each queued change is listed under it (Start the visit, Service report draft, Submit the service report).
  3. When the connection returns the changes are sent in order. A repeat of a change that already went through is recognised by its key and not applied twice.
  4. A change the server refuses stays on the stop, and on Changes made offline, as Refused with the reason. Correct the report reopens the form with your entries; Discard drops it.

See Field service: My day for what each step does.

Using the outbox

The page Changes made offline (opened from Review in the bar, or from see all on a work order) lists everything waiting on this device.

  1. Work normally while offline. Each labor entry, part or status change you record is added to the outbox, and the work order shows “Waiting to be sent” with what is queued.
  2. When you are back online, the app sends the outbox automatically: as soon as the connection returns, and again every minute. You can also choose Send now.
  3. Open Changes made offline to check. Each row shows when it was made, what it is (for example “1.5 h labor on 2026-10-23”, “Part used × 2”, “Start work”, “Start the visit”, “Submit the service report”), the work order or my day link, and its state: Waiting or Refused.
  4. For a Refused row, read the reason. If you can fix the cause (for example the work order was meanwhile cancelled or completed by someone else, or a rule was not met), choose Try again. If the change is no longer wanted, choose Discard; you are asked to confirm, and it will not be recorded.

The system sends changes in order, and a refusal of one does not stop the rest. A change is never sent twice: each carries a unique key, so if the network drops just as a change goes through, sending it again does not post it a second time. The server applies the same rules as if you had been online, so offline work never skips a check. For example, a status change that is not allowed from the work order's current status is refused with the reason.

Before you sign out, make sure the outbox is empty. Signing out removes the saved copies and any unsent changes from the device, and the system warns you first. See Signing out safely.

Using a tablet or phone

  • The layout adapts to narrow screens; the menu hides behind the Menu button.
  • Pick a larger text size and a roomier layout under How I like to work if you wear gloves or work at arm's length.
  • Your sign-in belongs to the browser tab. If the tablet is shared, sign out at the end of the shift (after the outbox is empty).
  • The offline copies and the outbox are kept per person on the device, so a colleague who signs in on the same tablet does not see yours.

Installing the app

You can install the platform as an app, so it opens in its own window from the home screen and starts even with no connection. Installing is optional; the app also works in a normal browser tab.

  1. Open the platform in your browser (Chrome or Edge on Android and desktop, Safari on iPad and iPhone) and sign in once while online.
  2. On Android or desktop, use the browser's Install app or Add to Home screen option (usually in the address bar or the browser menu). On an iPad or iPhone, choose Share and then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open it from the new icon. It is named Ops (“Operations Platform”).

The installed app keeps the page shell on the device, so it can start offline. It does not keep your data; your saved copies and outbox are the data on the device. After a software update, the new version is picked up the next time you open the app online.

Common mistakes

  • Opening a work order for the first time with no signal. It is not on the device yet. Open the day's work orders while you still have a connection.
  • Clearing the browser's site data while changes are waiting. That removes the outbox. Send first.
  • Using a different browser or device to look for an offline change. The outbox lives only on the device where you made the change.
  • Ignoring a Refused row. That hours or part never reached the record. Fix or discard it.

The saved copies and outbox are in the browser, so nothing needs setting up on the server. Hours reach the technician's timesheet through the work order once they are sent; see Time and expenses.
