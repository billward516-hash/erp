Payables is where supplier invoices (called bills in the Lite edition) are entered, checked and approved for payment. Open it from Finance → Payables. The page heading reads Supplier invoices.

What happens after approval depends on how your company does its accounting. With a connected accounting system, approved invoices are sent there and that system pays them. With the built-in books, approved invoices post to your books. Ask your administrator which applies to you.

Who can do this:

  • See supplier invoices: accounts payable clerks, bookkeepers, buyers, finance approvers, executives, district and property managers (permission "view payables").
  • Capture, approve, dispute, void and record payment: accounts payable clerks, finance approvers and bookkeepers (permission "manage payables"), for their own site.

The invoice list

Four tiles show Open payables, Overdue (the amount past its due date), Waiting for approval and Disputed. The table lists each invoice with supplier, number, site, purchase order, dates, amount and status. Overdue rows have a red edge and an Overdue badge. Untick Only unpaid to include paid and void invoices.

Capture an invoice

  1. Click Capture invoice. (In Lite the button and page say Enter a bill.)
  2. Choose the Supplier. In Lite you can add a new supplier on the same page with a code and name.
  3. In Standard, choose the Purchase order if there is one. The lines fill in from what was received and not yet invoiced, with a note showing the order price, quantity received and quantity already invoiced. If there is no order, choose the Facility and add lines by hand with Add a line not on the order.
  4. Enter the Invoice number and Invoice date. Leave Due date empty to use the supplier's payment terms.
  5. Check each line's quantity, unit price and account. For lines from an order the account comes from the order; for others the default is Repairs & maintenance.
  6. Enter Freight and Sales tax if the invoice shows them, and attach the invoice file (PDF or image) under Invoice file.
  7. Check the Invoice total against the paper and click Save invoice.

You can also start from a purchase order with Capture invoice on the order page.

The match check

On the invoice page every line shows Matches or Check. A banner Does not match the order lists the reasons. The system flags a line when:

  • the quantity invoiced so far (across all invoices that are not void) is more than the quantity received;
  • the unit price is more than 2% above the order price;
  • the line is not on the purchase order (in Standard; in Lite a bill without an order is normal).

Invoices for contractor work on a work order are also checked against the work order's not-to-exceed amount. See Purchasing for the order and receipt side.

Approve for payment

  1. Open an invoice that is Waiting for approval (or Disputed).
  2. If it matches, click Approve with the amount on the button.
  3. If it does not match, you must type Why should it be paid anyway? before the button is enabled. The note is kept on the invoice.

You cannot approve an invoice you captured yourself. The page says so and someone else has to approve it. In a one-person company the licence may allow a sole owner to approve their own bill; the audit trail then records it as self-approved.

Dispute or void

Type a reason (at least three letters) in the box at the bottom. Dispute is for an invoice that is waiting for approval and needs the supplier to correct something; a disputed invoice can be approved later. Void cancels an invoice that should not be paid or was entered by mistake. Neither can be used on a paid or void invoice.

Record payment

Where your company does not pay through a connected accounting system, an approved invoice shows a Record payment block. Enter the date and the check or ACH reference and click Mark paid with the amount. The full invoice total is recorded. If an accounting system pays your bills, the payment comes back with its next sync and you cannot type it here.

What the statuses mean

StatusMeaning
Waiting for approvalCaptured; a second person must approve it.
ApprovedCleared for payment. Not yet in the accounting system.
In accountingDelivered to the accounting system or posted to the built-in books.
PaidPayment recorded or received back from accounting.
DisputedHeld while the supplier fixes something.
VoidCancelled. It no longer counts toward what was invoiced against an order.

The Accounting block on the invoice shows the sync: Create bill or Void bill, which connection received it, its status, the reference in the accounting system and any last error. A MOCK badge means a demonstration connection, not a real system. Invoices waiting for approval and overdue invoices are included in reminders, and overdue ones appear on What is late. The Accounts payable aging report in Reports shows how overdue they are.

Lite and Standard

  • Lite: bills are entered line by line, with no purchase order. The match check does not treat a missing order as a problem. Approved bills post to the built-in books.
  • Standard: invoices are matched to purchase orders and receipts, and approved invoices go to the connected accounting system or the built-in books.

Common mistakes

  • Entering the same invoice twice. Search the list first; two entries double the quantity invoiced against the order and trip the match check.
  • Typing freight or tax into a line. Use the Freight and Sales tax fields so they post to the right accounts.
  • Approving a mismatch without understanding it. The note is the only record of why it was paid.

Approved invoices follow the accounting connection set under Accounting. With no connection, or a file-export connection, payments are typed in here. See Accounting connection and Books.
