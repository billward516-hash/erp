Purchasing follows one path: someone raises a purchase request, the right people approve it, a buyer turns it into a purchase order, the delivery is received, and the supplier's invoice is checked against the order and what actually arrived. That last check is called a three-way match: order, receipt and invoice must agree.

Purchasing is part of the Standard edition. The Lite edition has suppliers and bills but no purchase requests, orders or receiving; bills are entered without an order (see Payables).

Who can do this:

  • See requests, orders, suppliers and supplier performance: buyers, plant and maintenance managers, technicians, quality managers, finance approvers, executives and others (permission "view purchasing").
  • Raise a purchase request: technicians, maintenance and plant managers, supervisors, buyers (permission "request purchases").
  • Place orders, add suppliers and supplier prices, cancel orders, run quote requests: buyers (permission "manage purchasing").
  • Receive deliveries: buyers and maintenance managers (permission "receive goods").
  • Approve a request: whoever the sign-off route names, from the Approvals page.

Raise a purchase request

  1. Open Sales & supply → Purchasing and click New request.
  2. Choose the Facility, give it a Title (at least three letters), and set the Spend type: Operating (repairs, consumables) or Capital (asset, improvement).
  3. Optionally set Needed by and explain Why is this needed?
  4. Under Lines, choose a catalog part or describe something that is not a catalog part. Enter the quantity, the unit if it is not the part's stock unit, an estimated unit price and, if you know it, the supplier and the storeroom it goes into. For a catalog part, leaving the supplier empty means its preferred supplier; for a described item the buyer decides.
  5. Click Save draft.
  6. On the next screen, check the Sign-off route shown for the total, then click Submit for approval. Every line needs an estimated price first.

To restock, choose a facility next to Draft from reorder report on the Requests tab. It drafts a request for the parts below their reorder point. A request can also start from a work order that needs parts.

Request statuses

StatusMeaning
DraftStill being written. Edit it freely, then submit.
Awaiting approvalSigned off step by step; the route and who has signed are under Sign-offs.
ApprovedAll sign-offs are in. A buyer can now order it.
RejectedA signer said no. Change the request and resubmit.
OrderedEvery line is on a purchase order.
CancelledCancelled by the requester or a buyer, with a reason.

If you edit lines after the request has been submitted, the sign-offs are voided and the request goes back to draft. It must be approved again.

The requester and buyers can cancel a request that has no orders yet: type a reason (at least three letters) and click Cancel request.

Place the order

  1. Open an Approved request. If you can manage purchasing, the Place the order block appears.
  2. Check each line's Order price. You can change it to the price the supplier actually quoted. The block shows how many purchase orders will be created: one per supplier.
  3. Optionally set Expected delivery. If you leave it empty, the system uses the supplier's lead time for the part, or the request's needed-by date.
  4. Click Create purchase order.

Orders may come in up to 10% above the approved amount. Beyond that, edit the request so it goes back for approval. A line with no supplier shows a Choose a supplier… box in the Place the order block: pick an active supplier there. The choice is kept on the line and recorded in the audit trail, and it does not change the approved amount; orders are then created one per supplier. A line left without a supplier cannot be ordered, and the system tells you which one. Choosing suppliers when you raise the request is still better where you can, because editing lines on an approved request sends it back to draft for approval again.

Open the order to see its lines, then click PDF for the order document to send to the supplier. The platform does not email the order.

Receive a delivery

  1. Open the order from the Orders tab (tick Only open orders to narrow it).
  2. In the Receive delivery block, set Received now for each line. It starts at what is still outstanding. Lower it for a part delivery. Set it to 0 to skip a line.
  3. For a stocked part, choose Into storeroom, or Direct use (not stocked) for something consumed straight away. If the part is lot- or serial-tracked, enter its lots or serial numbers.
  4. Type the Packing slip number from the delivery.
  5. Click Record receipt.

Stock rises in the storeroom you chose and the receipt appears under Receipts with who received it and when. You cannot receive more than was ordered.

Check the supplier's invoice (three-way match)

When the invoice arrives, capture it from the order with Capture invoice (or from Payables). The invoice lines fill in from what was received and not yet invoiced. On the invoice, each line is marked Matches or Check:

  • A line is flagged if the quantity invoiced so far is more than the quantity received.
  • A line is flagged if the price is more than 2% above the order price.
  • A line is flagged if it is not on the order at all (unless the invoice is for contractor work on a work order, which is checked against the work order's not-to-exceed amount instead).

A person cannot approve an invoice they captured themselves. An invoice that does not match can still be approved, but the approver must type why it should be paid anyway. Details are on Payables.

Suppliers and prices

The Suppliers tab lists suppliers with their contact, payment terms, how many parts have prices and how many orders are open. Search by code or name. To add one, fill in the form under the table (code, name, order email, payment terms) and click Add supplier.

Open a supplier to see its scorecard, its Prices and its orders. To record a price, pick a part and enter the price, lead days and their part number, tick Preferred if this is your first choice, and click Save price. These prices pre-fill new requests and expected delivery dates.

Suppliers who do work for you can also be recorded as contractors: click Edit under Contractor to list their trades and the date their certificate of insurance expires. After that date the contractor cannot be sent work.

Supplier performance

The Supplier performance tab rates each supplier over the last 90 days: on-time delivery, rejected parts per million, supplier-related nonconformances and lines priced above the quote. The Rating is the worst of delivery, quality and unanswered corrective-action requests: Good, Watch, Poor or Too little data. Poor rows have a red edge.

A quality manager can open a supplier and click Ask for corrective action to raise a request addressed to that supplier. See CAPA and 8D.

Late orders and quote requests

Orders past their expected date show a red Late badge, buyers get reminders, and they appear on What is late. To compare suppliers before ordering, see Supplier requests for quote (the Quote requests tab on this page).

Common mistakes

  • Submitting before every line has a price. The submit is refused until every line has an estimated price.
  • Receiving into the wrong storeroom. Pick the storeroom before you click Record receipt; stock is counted where you say.
  • Recording a part delivery as complete. Lower Received now so the invoice check stays accurate.

A request can only be submitted if an approval rule matches its site, spend type and total; otherwise the screen says to ask an administrator to configure purchase approval rules. Make sure rules cover every site and both spend types. Related setup: Roles and access, Sites and people, Accounting connection.
