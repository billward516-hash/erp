The Safety page is for reporting anything that hurt someone, nearly hurt someone, damaged property or harmed the environment. Reporting a near miss is the cheapest way to fix a hazard. Anyone can report. The safety team then classifies the report, finds the root cause, assigns actions and closes it. Details about injured people are visible only to the safety team.

Who can do this:

  • Report something: nearly every role, including operators, technicians, store managers, quality staff and managers (permission "report safety"). You see your own reports under My reports.
  • See all incidents at a site: EHS/safety reviewers, plant and maintenance managers, facilities and district managers and executives (permission "view safety"). They do not see who was injured or the injury details.
  • Classify, investigate, assign actions, open a corrective action, close, and see injury details: EHS/safety reviewers (permission "manage safety").

Report something

Open Quality & safety → Safety. If you can only report, the page opens on Report something.

  1. Choose the kind: Near miss, First aid, Injury, Illness, Property damage or Environmental.
  2. Choose the Site (if you have more than one) and check When. The time can't be in the future.
  3. Enter Where (area, line, dock) and pick the equipment if any was involved.
  4. Under What happened (facts, not blame) describe it plainly.
  5. Optionally write What was done right away.
  6. Click Send report. The report opens. You can add photos and files.

You do not need to name the injured person. The safety team records who was involved and the details. The notification the safety team receives carries only the kind, place and report number, never private details.

Report first, tidy later. If someone is hurt, get help first, then report. Don't wait to know the cause.

Work an incident (safety team)

The Incidents tab shows three tiles: reports not yet classified, open reports, and near misses. Tick Only open to hide closed ones. Click a row to open it.

  1. Click Classify. Choose the severity (Low, Medium, High or Serious) and confirm the kind.
  2. For a person involved, choose the employee and enter the injury or illness (nature, body part), whether treatment beyond first aid was given, days away and days restricted, and whether the case is Recordable. Days away or restricted duty make a case recordable; the system refuses "no" in that case. Click Save classification.
  3. Click Record root cause. Enter the root cause and any contributing factors, and save.
  4. Under Actions, type the New action, pick an owner and due date, and click Add action. Owners find it on My actions, and the owner is told.
  5. For a problem that needs a deeper fix, click Open a corrective action. It opens linked to the incident. See CAPA and 8D.
  6. When every action is done or cancelled, click Close incident.

The system will not let you close an incident until it is classified, an injury or illness has a recordable decision, and a root cause is recorded (the only exception is a low-severity near miss or first-aid case), and no action is still open.

Who sees what

Everyone who can view safety sees the number, time, kind, place, equipment, severity and status. The person involved, injury description, treatment and days away or restricted are visible only to the safety team. Photos and files can be marked restricted so only the safety team and the person who uploaded them can open them. Reporters see a thank-you note when they open their own report.

My actions

The My actions tab lists the actions assigned to you across the Quality and Safety areas, with overdue ones marked. Type what was done and click Done.

Safety and the shift log

Incidents reported during a shift are listed in the safety box of that shift's log, and a safety item left open carries from shift to shift. See Shift log.

Common mistakes

  • Not reporting near misses. They are how problems get fixed before someone is hurt.
  • Writing blame instead of facts. Describe what happened. The investigation looks for causes.
  • Putting private details in the description. Leave names and injury details to the safety team, who record them where only they can see.

The safety team's access is controlled by role: give "manage safety" to the people who should see injury details. The reminder for unclassified incidents is configurable. Safety can be switched off per company. See Sites and people, Reminders and retention and Features and editions.
