When you need prices for something you buy, you can ask several suppliers at once. Create a quote request with the lines you need, choose the suppliers, record each supplier's answer as it comes in, compare them side by side and award each line. The award becomes a purchase request at the awarded prices.

The platform does not send anything to suppliers. The request letter is a PDF you download and email yourself. When suppliers reply, you type their answers in. Nothing is sent or received automatically.

Quote requests are part of the Standard edition, as part of Purchasing. The Lite edition has no purchasing.

Who can do this:

  • See quote requests: anyone who can view purchasing.
  • Create, send, record replies, award, cancel: buyers (permission "manage purchasing"), for their own site.

Create a quote request

  1. Open Sales & supply → Purchasing and click the Quote requests tab.
  2. Click New quote request.
  3. Choose the Site and give it a Title.
  4. Set Suppliers reply by (required before you can send) and, if useful, Goods needed by. Add Notes for the suppliers.
  5. Under Lines, pick a catalog part or choose Not a catalog part (describe it). Enter the quantity and, if you have one, a Target price. Click Add a line for more.
  6. Under Suppliers to ask, tick each supplier you want to invite. At least one is needed to send.
  7. Click Save draft.

Send it

  1. On the draft, click Request letter (PDF) and email it to each supplier yourself.
  2. Click Mark as sent. The request must have at least one supplier, a reply date and that date must not have passed. The status becomes Out for quotes.

To drop a request, click Cancel on a draft or sent request and give a reason.

Record the replies

  1. When a supplier answers, open the request. In the Suppliers table, click Enter their reply on their row.
  2. For each line, type the Unit price they quoted. Optionally add lead time in days, the minimum quantity and the date their price is valid until. Leave a line empty if they did not quote it.
  3. Enter Freight and Payment terms if they gave them.
  4. Click Save the reply. The supplier now shows Replied.
  5. If a supplier says no thanks, click They declined and say why if you know.

You can fix a mistake later with Change reply while the request is out for quotes.

Compare and award

Once at least one supplier has replied, a Comparison table appears with one row per line and one column per supplier.

  • The lowest price on each line is in bold and marked lowest. Under each price you see lead time, fastest where it is the quickest, the minimum quantity, and warnings: (below minimum) if your quantity is under their minimum and price expired if their valid-until date has passed.
  • The bottom rows total Goods, Freight and Landed total per supplier, and show the Slowest line. best marks the lowest landed total. A supplier who did not quote every line is flagged not every line, so their total is not directly comparable.
  1. In the Award to column, choose the supplier for each line you want to place now. Leave others at Not yet.
  2. If you pick a supplier who is not the lowest price, a box asks Why not the lowest price?. Give a reason such as lead time, quality, minimums or a preferred supplier. It is required.
  3. Click Award the chosen lines.

The system creates a draft purchase request at the awarded prices and opens it. Open it and click Submit for approval to send it through the normal approvals; the award itself does not approve spending. See Purchasing. Awarded lines show Awarded and cannot be awarded again. When every line is awarded, the request status becomes Awarded and links to the purchase request.

What the statuses mean

StatusMeaning
DraftBeing prepared. Not yet out.
Out for quotesMarked as sent; waiting for replies.
Replies lateOut for quotes and the reply date has passed.
AwardedEvery line has been awarded and a purchase request created.
CancelledDropped, with a reason.

The list shows Replies as "2 of 4" so you can see who has answered. The buyer who made the request is reminded on the reply date and every day after while suppliers are still outstanding. Late requests also show on What is late, and the Supplier performance and other purchasing reports are in Reports.

Common mistakes

  • Expecting the platform to email suppliers. Download the letter and send it yourself.
  • Comparing totals when a supplier skipped a line. Look for not every line before choosing the best total.
  • Forgetting to submit the purchase request. The award leaves it as a draft.
