"What is late" collects every date that has passed, across the whole platform, on a single page: money owed to you and by you, quotes and orders, work and maintenance, calibration and reviews. Use it as your morning check. Open Insight → What is late. It is in the menu for everyone.

Who sees what: each section appears only if you are allowed to see that kind of record, and only for the sites you can see. A bookkeeper sees invoices and bills; a technician sees work orders and preventive maintenance. Nobody sees a section they have no access to. If nothing at all is late for you, the page says "Nothing you can see is past its date."

How to read the page

  1. Read the sentence at the top. It gives the total number of late items and the four biggest groups, such as "14 items are past their date: 6 customer invoices past due; 4 work orders past their due date; …".
  2. Look at the tiles. They show up to five groups with the count and, where there is money, the total amount.
  3. Scroll to the section you care about. Each shows a count and an amount in its heading. Rows show the item, what is wrong, the owner, Days late and the amount.
  4. Click the item name to go to its record and fix it.

Each section shows the 40 most overdue items. If there are more, a note says "Showing the 40 most overdue of N". Fix the oldest and the list refills.

Group by owner

Tick Group by owner to flip the page. Instead of one section per kind of item, you get one block per person, biggest pile first, so a manager can see who has the most overdue. Items with no known owner appear under No owner recorded. The owner is the person who prepared or is responsible for the item, or for an invoice, the customer's account owner.

"Days late" counts calendar days from the due date to today.

What to do with it

  • Customer invoices: open Receivables, go to Collections and call.
  • Supplier bills: approve or dispute them in Payables.
  • Purchase orders and quote requests: chase the supplier from Purchasing or Supplier requests for quote.
  • Quotes: extend, revise or record the outcome. See Quotes and orders.
  • Customer requests: decide to bid or not, or quote. See Customer requests for quote.
  • Work, maintenance, calibration: see Work orders, Preventive maintenance and Quality.

Things to know

  • The page is live: it shows today's status each time you open it. It is not a saved report. For a dated snapshot you can export, use Reports.
  • Some links go to the list page for that area rather than the exact record, because several items share one list.
  • In the Lite edition, sections for areas Lite does not include (such as purchasing, quotes, work and production) have nothing to show.
  • You also get reminders by notification for many of these items. See Notifications and reminders.

Late dates come from each record. Reminder timing and who receives which reminder are set under Reminders. See Reminders and retention.
