# SC-007 Labs and cleanroom (semiconductor-adjacent) operations

**Requested by:** founder, 2026-10-24 ("make this appropriate for labs and clean room semiconductor adjacent operations").
**Status:** approved by the standing instruction to build queued work without waiting; the assumptions in (f) are recorded as decisions (D-184) and can be changed.

**Answer first.** The platform already covers most of what these sites run on: calibration with overdue blocking, document control with read-and-understood, change management and change notices, SPC, nonconformance and CAPA/8D, qualifications that gate who may do a task, lots with expiry, spares, and equipment maintenance. Four things a cleanroom or lab expects are missing: (A) environmental monitoring of rooms (particle counts by size, differential pressure, temperature, humidity) against class limits, (B) equipment qualification (IQ/OQ/PQ) with requalification before return to service and an equipment logbook, (C) chemicals and reagents controlled by hazard class, safety data sheet, storage compatibility and in-use (beyond-use) dates, and (D) samples and test results with chain of custody, specification checks, out-of-specification investigations and a certificate of analysis. Each is built on existing records, not beside them.

## (a) Workflow
- **A Cleanrooms.** A room has an ISO class (ISO 14644-1) and an occupancy state (at rest, operational), monitoring points, and sampling frequency. Particle counts are entered or imported by size (0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.5, 1, 5 µm) with differential pressure, temperature and humidity. Alert and action limits default from the ISO class and can be overridden per point. A reading over the alert limit notifies the room owner; over the action limit it raises a nonconformance (reusing quality) and shows on What is late; repeated alerts trend. A trend page and an environmental monitoring report (compliance by room and period, excursions, overdue sampling) cover audits.
- **B Qualification and logbook.** A qualification protocol (IQ, OQ, PQ or requalification) attaches to an asset, links its controlled documents, is executed and approved by different people through the approval engine, and sets a requalification due date. A completed repair or a change on a qualified tool marks it "requalification required"; return to service is refused until a passing protocol is recorded. An equipment logbook shows maintenance, state changes, calibrations, qualifications and changes in time order. Tool states are reported in SEMI E10 style groupings (productive, standby, engineering, scheduled downtime, unscheduled downtime, non-scheduled) mapped from the existing asset states.
- **C Chemicals and reagents.** Parts can be marked as chemicals with hazard classes (GHS), a safety data sheet document and a storage group. Containers carry lot, opened date and beyond-use date (opened date plus the part's in-use days, capped by expiry). Putting a container in a location is refused when the location holds an incompatible storage group. Reagent and standard preparations record what lots went in and the new expiry. Alerts for expiry and beyond-use, a hazardous inventory report by location and hazard class against a limit per location, and a "no SDS" exception list.
- **D Samples and tests.** A sample is received (customer optional), gets custody events (who, when, where), is assigned tests from a method list (method document, analyte, unit, specification limits). A result is entered by an analyst qualified for that method, using instruments in calibration; a second person reviews. A result outside specification opens an out-of-specification investigation that must be closed (and can raise a CAPA) before the sample report is released. A certificate of analysis PDF lists results against limits and the instruments and methods used. Turnaround time is tracked and overdue samples show on What is late. Retention and disposal dates remind.

## (b) Value
For a cleanroom: proof of environmental control for auditors and customers, excursions that cannot be missed, tools that cannot return to production unqualified. For a lab: results traceable to analyst, method, instrument calibration and sample, with out-of-specification results forced through an investigation. For both: chemicals that cannot silently expire or be stored beside an incompatible one.

## (c) Reused
Assets and states, meters and conditions, calibration records and overdue blocking, documents and read-and-understood, qualifications gating, approvals and delegation, MOC and change notices, nonconformance and CAPA/8D, SPC and charts, lots and expiry, spares, reminders catalogue and daily job, What is late, attachments, report registry and exports, imports pipeline, features registry and editions, audit.

## (d) New
Rooms, monitoring points, limits tables and readings; particle-counter file import; excursion handling and the monitoring report. Qualification protocols and results, the requalification gate, the equipment logbook view, E10-style state report. Chemical attributes, containers, preparations, storage-group rule and the hazardous inventory report. Samples, custody, methods, tests, results, review, out-of-specification investigations, certificate of analysis and sample reports.

## (e) Native vs integration
Native, file based. Particle counters and building monitoring systems that export CSV are read through the import pipeline; no live instrument, LIMS or building-system connection is built (they are named as follow-ups when a customer names the product). The ISO 14644-1 class limits are generated from the standard's formula and shown so the company can confirm them against its own copy of the standard; the platform does not certify compliance. Regulatory submissions (21 CFR Part 11 validation, ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation) are not claimed: the platform provides the records; the lab owns its validation. Electronic signatures reuse the existing optional e-signature feature.

## (f) Assumptions recorded as decisions (change on request)
- Packaging: four feature keys (`cleanroom`, `qualification`, `chemicals`, `lab_samples`), Standard edition, not in Lite, each switchable on its own in Features; a "Lab or cleanroom" choice in the fit interview turns the right ones on.
- Default limits come from ISO 14644-1 class limits for the named class; alert is 80% of action unless set.
- A sample result must be reviewed by someone other than the person who entered it (unless the company's single-person approval setting allows it).
- Beyond-use date = opened date + in-use days, never later than expiry.
- Storage compatibility is by company-defined storage groups with an incompatible-pairs list; the platform ships an editable starter list, not a certified one.

## (g) Tests
Class limit generation against known ISO 14644-1 values; excursion thresholds and notification dedupe; nonconformance raised once per excursion; import of a particle-counter file; separation of duties on qualification and result review; requalification gate on return to service; logbook ordering; E10 grouping; storage-group refusal and beyond-use calculation; hazardous inventory totals; out-of-specification flow blocking release; certificate content; calibration and analyst-qualification gating; tenant and site isolation for every new table; schema-index guard.

## Not in this change
Live integrations (counters, building systems, LIMS, instruments), wafer lot tracking and recipes (a manufacturing-execution scope), compliance certification of any kind, waste manifests and regulatory filings, GS1 and instrument barcodes beyond the existing scan station.
