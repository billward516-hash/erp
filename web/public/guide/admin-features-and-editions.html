    Administrator brief

    Features and editions

    Hide or show areas of the system by role, and understand what the Lite and Standard editions and the per-site licences include.

    Administrator brief: more technical, written for the person who sets the system up.

    On this page
    • The Features page
    • Lite and Standard
    • Licences and entitlements
    • Common mistakes

    Two separate controls decide which parts of the system a person can use. Features are yours: you choose, area by area, who sees it. Editions and licences are the commercial limits that come with the plan the company bought: the edition (Lite or Standard), caps on properties and stores, and whether field service is licensed for a site. Both apply at once: a feature has to be allowed by the licence and shown to the person.

    Only an administrator sees Admin → Features. Editions and licences are not changed on any screen; the platform operator changes them (see Licences).

    The Features page

    Open Admin → Features. It lists all 34 features in seven groups (My day, Company, Maintenance, Production, Quality & safety, Sales & supply, Finance). At the top it tells you how many are limited or turned off. Each feature has a selector with three choices:

    ChoiceWhat happens
    Everyone (the default)Everyone whose roles allow it sees the feature.
    Only some rolesYou tick the roles that see it (each shows how many people hold it). People without a ticked role do not see it.
    Nobody (hidden)Nobody sees it. It remains on this page so you can bring it back.

    Change a feature

    1. Find the feature and change its selector. If you chose Only some roles, tick at least one role (the Save button stays off until you do).
    2. Read the yellow note if one appears. It says what else is affected (for example, hiding Purchasing means planning and reorder suggestions create purchase requests nobody will see).
    3. Choose Save. The row shows when it was changed and by whom. The setting applies at once for new page loads (it is cached for at most 30 seconds).

    What “hidden” does

    • The feature's permissions are taken away from the roles it is hidden from, so every existing check hides or refuses it.
    • Its menu entries disappear, and the system refuses its pages, data and reports for someone who can see it nowhere. They get “<Feature> is turned off for your role. An administrator can turn it on under Admin → Features.” (error feature_off, status 403).
    • Records are not deleted and nothing is lost. Turn the feature back on and everything is as it was.
    • People are not asked to approve records of a feature they cannot see (expenses, purchase requests, quotes, changes, temporary changes, FMEA, continuity plans and business cases).
    • A reminder belonging to a feature that is off for everyone is not sent. The Reminders page says so.
    • The administrator is treated like everyone else: if you restrict a feature to some roles and leave out Administrator, you will not see it either, though you can still change the setting here.

    Home, approvals, notifications, reports and the administration screens are always available and are not features.

    Features that lean on each other

    • Assets hidden: only the register pages go; work orders, PM and production still use assets.
    • Work orders hidden: PM and the shift log rely on them.
    • Storerooms and lots hidden: receiving into stock, kits and production issues need them.
    • Chemicals and reagents hidden: receiving a chemical part still makes its containers; only the screens, alerts and reports go.
    • Production hidden: planning works from production routings.
    • Quality hidden: FMEA and SPC live on the Quality page.
    • Time and timesheets hidden: safety incident rates use timesheet hours.
    • Training and qualifications hidden: qualification checks on work orders and machines still apply, but nobody can grant new qualifications.
    • Customers and Purchasing hidden: invoices and bills still choose customers and suppliers; only their own pages go.
    • Books hidden: postings continue while the built-in books are the accounting connection.

    Let the fit interview suggest settings

    The fit interview (Admin → Fit interview) recommends features from answers about the business. Its button Hide the features we did not recommend sets those features to Nobody (hidden) in one go. It is reversible here. See Implementation planner.

    Lite and Standard

    The edition is part of the licence and applies to the whole company. Both editions use the same records, so moving from one to the other migrates nothing.

    LiteStandard
    Sites1As licensed (properties up to 100, stores up to 5)
    Active users5As licensed
    News, people directory, handbook and policies, searchYesYes
    Expense reportsYes; the owner approvesYes; routed by department, site and amount
    Customers, simple invoices, payments, collectionsYesYes, plus quotes, sales orders and shipments
    Suppliers and billsYes (bills without purchase orders)Yes, plus purchasing and three-way checks
    Built-in booksYesYes, or an external accounting system
    Data import (bank statements, customers, suppliers, history)YesYes
    ReportsProfit and loss, balance sheet, receivables aging, payables aging, expense summaryAll
    Assets, maintenance, PM, storerooms, production, quality, safety, planning, budgets, leasing, store operations, field serviceNot includedYes (subject to the feature settings and licences)
    • In Lite, anything outside its areas answers with: “This is part of the Standard edition. Your company uses Lite (books, invoicing, bills, expenses, policies and the intranet); an administrator can upgrade.” (error edition, status 403).
    • Lite lets one person approve their own expense report, bill or reimbursement when they are the only person who could sign. The audit trail records it as self-approved. The moment someone else could sign, the normal rule applies again.
    • Prices are commercial matters and are not in the software; the software only enforces what each edition includes.

    Not in any edition: payroll processing, tax filing, multi-currency, consolidation, fixed-asset depreciation schedules and inventory valuation in the books.

    Moving from Lite to Standard

    The platform operator changes the edition. Then keep the built-in books (Standard modules post through them the same way), or move to an external accounting system: export the journal, import opening balances into the new system, disconnect the books under Admin → Accounting and connect the new one. See Accounting connection.

    Licences and entitlements

    LicenceWhat it limitsWhat you see when you hit it
    Property capActive sites of kind Property (up to 100).409 “your plan covers up to N properties and N are active; deactivate a property you no longer manage to add another”. The Properties licensed tile on the Sites page turns red at the limit.
    Store capActive sites of kind Store (up to 5).409 with the same wording for stores.
    User cap and site cap (Lite)Five active users, one site.A message beginning “licence: N of N users are active, the most the lite edition includes; disable a user or upgrade”.
    Field service, per siteService agreements, visits, dispatch, technicians and vehicles, at each site that has the field_service entitlement.402 module_not_licensed: “Field service is not licensed for this site”. The Field service menu entries appear only when at least one site has it.

    There is no screen to buy or switch on an entitlement. The operator records it for each site with npx tsx scripts/set-entitlement.ts --company <slug> --field-service on --site <code> (or --all-sites; --list shows what is on, --edition lite|standard changes the edition, and --help explains everything). Each change is recorded in the audit trail. If someone sees a 402 error, check the site they are working at: field service may be licensed at one site and not another.

    Common mistakes

    • Hiding a feature and then wondering why a report is empty or an approval has vanished. Check Admin → Features first.
    • Choosing Only some roles and forgetting the Administrator role.
    • Confusing “hidden” with “no permission”. A hidden feature gives 403 feature_off; a missing permission gives 403 forbidden. Both are explained in FAQ and troubleshooting.
