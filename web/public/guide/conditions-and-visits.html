    Quality and safety

    Conditions and visits

    Log cooler and freezer temperatures against limits, and walk a site with a checklist so every failure becomes a fix.

    On this page
    • Conditions: readings against limits
    • Visits: walking a site with a checklist
    • Checklists
    • Common mistakes

    This page has two jobs for sites that must prove they keep standards: condition logs (temperature or similar readings from coolers, freezers and other equipment, checked against limits) and site visits (a manager walks a site with a checklist, and each failed item turns into a repair request or a task). It is used by retail, food service and property sites, and by any plant with a cooler or a clean room to watch.

    • See conditions and visits: store and site managers, district managers, facilities and property managers, quality managers, technicians and executives (permission "view visits").
    • Log a reading: people who can record equipment state at the site (technicians, operators, store managers and others; permission "record equipment state").
    • Set the limits on a piece of equipment: asset managers (permission "manage assets"), from the equipment's page.
    • Conduct a visit and manage the checklists: people with "conduct site visits", typically store/site managers, facilities and property managers and plant managers.

    Open Quality & safety → Conditions & visits. The tabs are Conditions, Visits and, for people who conduct visits, Checklists.

    Conditions: readings against limits

    The Conditions tab lists every logged point, with the site, the equipment, its limits (and how often it should be read), the last reading, and its state. Tiles show how many points are logged, how many are out of limits now, and how many readings are overdue. Rows that are not OK are highlighted.

    StateMeaning
    okThe last reading is within limits and a reading is not overdue.
    out since …The last reading is outside the limits. The worst value of the excursion is shown in red.
    missedNo reading for one and a half times the expected interval.
    no readingsSet up but nothing logged yet.

    Log a reading

    1. Find the equipment's row. (You can also scan the equipment's QR code to land on its page; the same form is there.)
    2. Type the value in the box, using the unit shown.
    3. Optionally add a Note / action taken.
    4. Click Log. A message tells you the result.

    If the value is outside the limits, an excursion opens and one maintenance request is raised and maintenance managers are told: "Out of limits: maintenance has been asked to check it." A cooler that stays warm for six readings raises one request, not six; further readings are added to the same excursion. When a reading is back inside the limits, the excursion closes. See Work orders for what maintenance does with the request.

    Set up limits

    1. Open the cooler or freezer from Maintenance → Assets (see Assets).
    2. In the Condition log box, click Log temperature (or Edit limits).
    3. Enter the unit (°F, °C, %RH), a low limit, a high limit (one or both; low must be below high), and how many hours between readings.
    4. Click Save.

    The asset page also shows the last seven days of readings and recent excursions, including the worst value and the maintenance work order.

    Visits: walking a site with a checklist

    The Visits tab lists past visits with the site, checklist, date, who visited, the score and the number of open fixes. A visit with a critical failure is highlighted.

    Do a visit

    1. On the Visits tab choose the Site and the Checklist, then click Start visit. The visit keeps its own copy of the checklist, so changing the checklist later does not alter it.
    2. Go through the items. Each shows its area. A critical tag marks an item that matters most. A failed last time too tag marks a repeat failure.
    3. For a good item click Pass. If it does not apply, click n/a.
    4. For a failure, type what is wrong in If it fails: what is wrong (required). If it is about a piece of equipment or a space, pick it in the list. Otherwise leave Not equipment (an action). Then click Fail.
    5. To change an answer before you finish, click change.
    6. When every item is answered, add a Summary for the store. If any failed item was not about equipment, choose Who fixes the rest…. Click Complete visit.

    What happens when you complete the visit:

    • The score is passes divided by passes plus failures. "n/a" items are left out. The score and the number of critical failures show on the visit and on the list.
    • Each failure about equipment or a space becomes a maintenance request (high severity if the item is critical, otherwise medium).
    • Each other failure becomes an action for the person you chose, due the next day if critical, otherwise in a week. It appears on their My actions tab (see Quality).
    • If anything failed, the people at the site who can request work orders are told, with the score and the first failures.

    The visit page shows the previous visit's score for comparison, and each failed item shows its follow-up: the maintenance request or work order, or the action's status.

    Checklists

    People who conduct visits can create and edit checklists on the Checklists tab.

    1. Click New checklist (or Edit on an existing one).
    2. Give it a short Code and a Name.
    3. Type one item per line, written as Area: item. Start a line with ! to mark it critical. For example: 
      Front: Entrance clean and doors working
! Cold chain: Coolers in range, logs complete
    4. Click Save checklist.

    A checklist needs at least one item. The list shows how many items each checklist has and how many are critical.

    Common mistakes

    • Logging nothing, then everything at once. Missed readings are flagged. Read at the interval set for the equipment.
    • Not writing what is wrong. A failed item can't be saved without a note, and the note becomes the repair request.
    • Failing equipment without picking it. If you don't pick the equipment, the failure becomes an action for a person, not a maintenance request.
    • Waiting to start the visit until you are back in the office. Do it as you walk; a tablet works.

    Visits and condition logs are part of the Standard edition and can be switched off or limited by role. Two reminders support them: "Missed condition readings" and "Store walks not done" (default: a site with walks and none completed in 31 days). Give the visit and record-state permissions to the right roles at each site. See Features and editions, Reminders and retention and Sites and people.
