    Sales and supply

    Customer requests for quote

    Record each request a customer sends you, decide whether to bid, and answer it on time with a quote.

    On this page
    • The list and the due board
    • Record a request
    • Decide and answer
    • What the statuses mean
    • Reminders and what is late
    • Common mistakes

    Customers often send a request for quote (an RFQ) by email with drawings or a spec attached. This page is where you log each one, so the reply date is visible to the whole sales team and nothing is forgotten. You then decide whether to bid, build the quote from the request, and let the status follow the quote.

    The platform does not read your email. You record each request by hand when it arrives. It is part of the Standard edition, together with quotes and orders.

    • See requests and the due board: anyone who can view sales (sales representatives and managers, plant managers, quality managers, finance approvers, billing clerks, bookkeepers, executives).
    • Record, decide, build the quote, change the date, close a request: sales representatives, sales managers and service dispatchers (permission "quote"), for their own site.

    The list and the due board

    Open Sales & supply → Quotes & orders and click the Customer requests tab. Four tiles at the top count requests by reply date:

    • Past the reply date
    • Due today
    • Due in 7 days
    • Waiting for bid or no-bid: requests nobody has decided on yet.

    The table lists each request with the customer, their reference, the subject, the date a reply is due, the days left, the owner and the status. Late rows have a red edge. Tick Only open requests to hide finished ones.

    Record a request

    1. On the Customer requests tab, click Record a request.
    2. Choose the Customer and, if you know who sent it, the Contact.
    3. Type Their reference (the customer's own RFQ number) so you can quote it back to them.
    4. Choose How it arrived: email, portal, phone, meeting or other. Set Received on and Reply due by. The reply date cannot be earlier than the received date.
    5. Choose the Quoting site.
    6. Enter a Subject (at least three letters) and describe What they are asking for.
    7. Click Record request. On the next page, add the files the customer sent under Files from the customer.

    You become the owner of the request. The owner receives the reminders.

    Decide and answer

    Open a request. The tiles show the reply date, when it arrived, the owner and the linked quote. While the request is open, the What to do block offers these actions.

    1. To commit, click We will bid. The status changes to Bidding.
    2. To decline, type a Reason (at least three letters) and click No bid. The reason is required so you can later see patterns such as too small, outside our range or no capacity.
    3. To answer with a price, click Build the quote. The quote form opens with the customer already chosen. Enter lines, validity and notes as described in Quotes and orders and click Save draft. The request is now linked to that quote and moves to Bidding if it was not already. The button then reads Quote already started.
    4. If the customer takes the request back, type a reason and click Customer withdrew it.
    5. If the reply date moves, pick the new date under Move the reply date and click Change date.

    You do not set Quoted, Won or Lost yourself. They follow the quote, so the outcome is only entered once.

    What the statuses mean

    StatusMeaning
    Needs a decisionRecorded; nobody has chosen to bid or not.
    BiddingYou decided to bid, or a quote has been started but not yet sent.
    No bidYou chose not to quote. The reason is kept.
    QuotedThe linked quote has been sent to the customer.
    WonThe linked quote was accepted.
    LostThe linked quote was declined.
    WithdrawnThe customer withdrew the request.
    Reply late (red badge)The reply date has passed while the request is still Needs a decision or Bidding.

    Reminders and what is late

    Every morning the owner of each open request is told two days before the reply date, on the day, and every day after it until the request is answered or closed. Requests past their date also appear on What is late. The report Customer quote requests in Reports shows reply times across all requests.

    Common mistakes

    • Leaving a request at "Needs a decision". Decide quickly. A request nobody has decided on is the most likely to be missed.
    • Building the quote outside the request. Start it with Build the quote so the two stay linked and the status updates itself.
    • Forgetting the customer's reference. Without it, reconciling your reply to their system is harder.

    Customer requests are recorded by hand; there is no email intake until your company chooses a sending and receiving domain. Reminders go to the request owner by the daily job; see Scheduled jobs and Reminders and retention.
