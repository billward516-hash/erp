    Sales and supply

    Customers

    Keep one record per customer with contacts, opportunities and follow-ups, and start quotes from it.

    On this page
    • Find a customer
    • Add a customer
    • Contacts, opportunities and activity
    • The pipeline
    • My follow-ups
    • Other things on the customer page
    • Lite and Standard
    • Common mistakes

    The customer record is where sales work starts. It holds the account details, the people you deal with, the deals you are chasing and a log of every call and email. Quotes, orders and invoices all hang off a customer, so a clean record here saves time everywhere else.

    • See customers, the pipeline and follow-ups: sales representatives and managers, bookkeepers, billing clerks, finance approvers, plant managers, quality managers, executives, dispatchers and field technicians (permission "view customers").
    • Add or change customers, contacts, opportunities and activities: sales representatives, sales managers and bookkeepers (permission "manage customers").
    • Start a quote from a customer: sales representatives, sales managers and service dispatchers (permission "quote").

    Find a customer

    Open Sales & supply → Customers. The page has three tabs: Customers, Pipeline and My follow-ups.

    On Customers, type part of a name or code into Search. Each row shows the code, name, type, industry, owner, how many opportunities are open and the date of the last activity. Click a row to open the customer.

    TypeWhat it means
    ProspectSomeone you are talking to who has not bought yet. Every new customer you add by hand starts here.
    CustomerA prospect that has accepted a quote. The record changes to Customer by itself when you record the acceptance (see Quotes and orders).

    Add a customer

    1. On the Customers page, click New customer. You only see the button if you can manage customers.
    2. Enter a Code (a short unique label such as ACME) and the Name. Both are required.
    3. Fill in Industry, Phone, Website and Billing address if you have them. Payment terms starts as Net 30; change it if this customer has different terms. New invoices and quotes take their terms from here.
    4. Click Save customer. The new record opens.

    If you already keep a customer list in a spreadsheet or another system, ask your administrator to bring it in with Data import instead of typing each one. See Importing data.

    Contacts, opportunities and activity

    The customer page is laid out in blocks. If you can manage customers, each block has a small form under its table.

    Contacts

    1. Under Contacts, enter the person's name (required), and optionally title, email and phone.
    2. Tick Primary for the person who should receive invoices and payment reminders. The reminder email on a receivable goes to the primary contact's email.
    3. Click Add contact.

    Opportunities

    An opportunity is a possible sale: a title, an amount, and the date you expect to close it.

    1. Under Opportunities, type a title (at least three letters), an amount and an expected close date.
    2. Click Add. It starts at the Lead stage.
    3. As the deal moves, pick the next stage in the row and click Move. If you choose Lost, type why in the Why lost? box.
    StageStarting chance of winning
    Lead10%
    Qualified25%
    Proposal50%
    Negotiation75%
    Won100%
    Lost0%

    When you accept a quote that is linked to an opportunity, the opportunity moves to Won by itself. Won and lost opportunities no longer show a stage selector.

    Activity

    Use Activity to keep a record of what happened. Choose the kind (Call, Email, Meeting, Note or Follow-up task), write a short summary and click Log. A follow-up task also needs a due date. If the customer has an open opportunity you can tie the entry to it.

    The pipeline

    The Pipeline tab lists all open opportunities in four columns: Lead, Qualified, Proposal and Negotiation. The line above the columns shows how many are open, the total value, and the value weighted by probability: each amount multiplied by its chance of winning. Click a card to open its customer.

    My follow-ups

    The My follow-ups tab lists your open follow-up tasks with their due dates. Overdue ones have a red edge. Click Done when you have finished one. The task owner is also reminded when a follow-up falls due.

    Other things on the customer page

    • Quotes and orders: lists of this customer's quotes and sales orders (Standard edition only). Click one to open it. New quote in the page header starts a quote for this customer.
    • Specifications: if your company stores this customer's drawings or standards in document control, they are listed here with the revision currently in force. See Document control.
    • Account: open invoices, payments, credit limit and exposure are on the customer's account page under Receivables. See Receivables.

    Lite and Standard

    Customers, contacts, opportunities and follow-ups work the same in both editions. In the Lite edition the quote and order blocks do not appear, because quotes, sales orders and shipments are part of Standard.

    Common mistakes

    • Duplicate customers. Search by name before adding. Two records for the same company split their invoices and credit exposure.
    • No primary contact. Without one, the Email payment reminder button on an invoice is unavailable.
    • Forgetting to log a lost reason. The reason is the only record of why you did not win the work.

    Administrators can import customer lists and set credit limits and owners in bulk. Roles that may manage customers are set per role; see Roles and access and Importing data.
