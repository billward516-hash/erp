    Field service

    Field service: My day

    The technician's tablet route for the day: start a visit, fill in the service report, and get the customer's signature.

    On this page
    • Your route
    • Start a visit
    • Fill in the service report
    • Without a connection
    • What happens when you submit
    • What the stop statuses mean
    • Tools and truck stock
    • Common mistakes

    My day is the screen a field technician uses on a tablet in the truck and at the customer's site. It shows your stops for the day in order, with the time to arrive, the customer's address, access notes and the equipment. When you start a visit you fill in the service report. Submitting it completes the job and sends it for billing.

    Who can do this: field technicians (permission "execute field service"). You must also be set up as a technician by your dispatcher, or the page says "You are not set up as a technician. Ask your dispatcher." Field service must be licensed for your site.

    Open Field service → My day. Your dispatcher's side of this is described in Field service.

    Your route

    Use the arrows or the date box to move between days. Each stop is a card showing:

    • The arrival time and customer, and the status of the stop (scheduled, dispatched, in progress or completed).
    • What the job is and the equipment tag and name.
    • The site and address, drive time from the previous stop (marked "estimate" if the system could not use map coordinates), and the expected minutes on site.
    • Access notes in a yellow box, for example where to park or who to ask for.
    • The make, model and serial number of the equipment.

    "Nothing is scheduled for you on this day" means you have no visits assigned. You are told in the app when a visit is assigned to you.

    Start a visit

    1. Find the stop. Its button says Start the visit while it is scheduled or dispatched.
    2. Tap Start the visit when you arrive. The report form opens and the stop turns to in progress.

    Once a visit is in progress, the button on the card reads Service report (or Hide the report when the form is open). A finished stop shows View report.

    Fill in the service report

    Work from the top of the form to the bottom. Everything is large enough to use with a finger.

    1. Checklist. If the visit has a checklist, tap OK, Issue or N/A for every item. For an Issue, type what is wrong. You must answer every item before you can submit.
    2. Readings. Tap Add a reading for measurements such as amps or pressure. Give the name, the value and the unit.
    3. Work performed. Describe what you did (at least five characters). This is required.
    4. Findings. Anything you noticed.
    5. Hours on site. Enter hours in quarter-hour steps. This is required and must be more than zero and no more than 24.
    6. The problem is fixed. On a call-out, tick this when you solved it.
    7. Parts used (from the truck). Tap Add a part, choose the part and quantity. Parts come off your truck's stock, and cannot go below zero.
    8. Other work the customer needs. Tap Add a recommended repair for anything else you found. Describe it, choose Now, Soon or When convenient, and enter a price if you know it. Each becomes a line on a draft quote that the office can send.
    9. Customer sign-off. If the customer is there, leave The customer is here to sign ticked, type their name and have them sign in the box. If they are not, untick it and say why (not on site, refused, locked out).
    10. Tap Submit report.

    Tap Save draft at any point. The draft is kept on the server (or, with no connection, on the tablet and sent later) and also on the tablet as you type. When you reopen the report, after a reload, a closed tab or on another tablet, your entries are put back in the form: checklist answers, readings, work performed, findings, hours, parts, recommended repairs and the signature. The page says Draft restored.

    About the signature

    If the customer is present, both a typed name (at least two characters) and a drawn signature are required. If they are not, a short reason is required instead. The signature image is stored with the report and shown to the office as View signature. A signature saved with a draft stays with it when you reopen the report; draw again only to replace it.

    Without a connection

    My day keeps working when the signal drops, in a basement or at a remote site. While you are online the tablet keeps a copy of your day, of each visit's details and report draft, and of the parts list. Open My day once with a connection before you set off.

    • See the day and the details. The page says Copy saved on this device [time]: no connection right now. Visit details opens the number, customer, agreement, due date, access notes, notes and checklist of a stop.
    • Start a visit. Start the visit works as usual: the stop turns to in progress with a Waiting to send tag, and the time you tapped is kept as your arrival time.
    • Fill in the report. Labor hours, parts, notes, readings, findings, recommended repairs and the customer's signature all work. Save draft says Draft saved on this device. The form checks the same things the server will (every checklist item answered, work performed, hours, name and signature or a reason) so a missing item is shown at once and not hours later.
    • Submit. Submit report closes the form and shows the stop as completed and waiting to send. The visit is not billed and the office does not see the report until it is sent.

    Each change waits in order (start, draft, submission) and goes to the server when the connection returns, on its own, within a minute. Each carries a unique key, so if the network dropped just as a change went through, sending it again changes nothing a second time: the labor and parts are posted once. See Working offline and on tablets for the outbox.

    If the server refuses a change (the dispatcher cancelled or reassigned the visit, the truck did not have enough of a part), the stop shows Refused with the reason. Choose Correct the report to reopen the form with your entries still in it, fix it and submit again (the new attempt replaces the refused one), choose Try again if the cause is gone, or Discard to drop it. Nothing is dropped silently.

    What happens when you submit

    • Your hours are recorded as labor on the visit's work order.
    • Your parts are taken off your truck's stock and recorded on the work order.
    • Checklist items marked Issue are added to the findings.
    • The work order is marked completed. A second person with verify rights checks it later, as for any work order.
    • The visit becomes Done and the report is marked for billing: ready to invoice, or covered by the customer's agreement.
    • If you listed recommended repairs, the dispatchers are notified to quote them.

    After submitting you cannot change the report. Tell your dispatcher if something is wrong.

    What the stop statuses mean

    Status shownMeaning
    scheduledAssigned to you for this day.
    dispatchedYour dispatcher has sent the day. It behaves the same as scheduled.
    in progressYou started it. The report can be filled in.
    completedThe report is submitted.

    Tools and truck stock

    Tools are checked out and returned by the dispatcher or by you on the dispatcher's Technicians, trucks and tools tab if you have access. A tool that is overdue for calibration cannot be checked out. See Field service: technicians, trucks and tools.

    Common mistakes

    • Submitting without answering every checklist item or without hours. The form tells you what is missing.
    • Choosing a part you did not carry. The system takes it from your truck's stock and refuses if the truck does not have enough.
    • Forgetting that the customer must sign, or that a reason is needed when they do not.
    • Leaving a report open overnight with only a draft saved. A draft does not complete the visit.
    • Signing out of the tablet while changes are still waiting. The outbox and the saved copies are removed; the page warns you first.
    • Opening a visit for the first time with no signal. Load My day while you are online so the details are on the tablet.

    Administrators: to use this page a person needs the Field Technician role and a technician profile (skills, working hours, optional truck and home coordinates), and the site needs the field service licence. See Field service, Features and editions and Offline and tablets. My day works offline using the same saved copies and outbox as work orders; nothing is set up on the server for it.
