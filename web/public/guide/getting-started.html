    Getting started

    Sign in, find your way around the menu, search, save your work safely and sign out.

    On this page
    • Signing in

    This page takes you from the sign-in screen to a confident first day: how to get in, what the menu is telling you, where to look for what needs your attention, and how the system protects the things you type.

    Signing in

    Most companies use single sign-on: you sign in with the same company account you use for email, so there is no separate password for this system.

    1. Open the address your company gave you. The page is titled Sign in.
    2. Type your work email in Work email.
    3. Choose Continue with your company account. You go to your company's own sign-in page; sign in there as usual.
    4. You come back to the platform and land on Home. If you opened a link to a particular page before signing in, you are taken to that page instead.

    If the page says your company or email is not set up, or that you are not a user, ask your administrator. Signing in does not create an account; someone has to add you first.

    Signing in with an issued token

    Below the button there is a fold-out called Development sign-in with an API token. A token is a long code, beginning erp_, that stands in for a password. Your administrator may hand one out for trials and demonstrations, or for a device that cannot use company sign-in. Paste it into API token and choose Sign in with token. Each token signs you in as one specific person, so never share yours.

    Tokens can be switched off for the company once single sign-on works, and single sign-on is set up per company. See Administrator brief: sign-in.

    If you see “Your session has ended. Sign in again.”, the system no longer recognises your sign-in. This is normal after you sign out in another window, or after your sign-in expires. Sign in again; anything you had typed into a form is waiting for you (see Saving and autosaved drafts).

    Why some menu items are missing

    If a page described in this guide is not in your menu, one of four things is true. They are checked in this order:

    1. Your company has hidden it from your role. Companies can switch features on or off for each role. A hidden feature leaves the menu, and if you open its address directly you see Not available: “This part of the platform is turned off for your role.” An administrator can turn it on under Admin → Features.
    2. Your edition does not include it. The Lite edition covers the intranet, policies, expenses, customers and invoicing, suppliers and bills, the built-in books, data import and their reports. Maintenance, production, quality, safety and the rest are Standard. In Lite the menu shows only those areas, and the top-left name reads “Intranet · Lite”. See not licensed.
    3. Your role does not allow it. Each page needs a permission. For example Books needs the right to see the books, and Time shows if you are linked to an employee record or you approve or export time. See Roles and access.
    4. It is not licensed for your site. Field service appears only where field service has been licensed.

    You do not need to know which reason applies. Ask your administrator, and say which menu entry you expected. They can see your roles and your features.

    Home

    Home is the first page after sign-in: company news, a My work list of what is waiting for you, upcoming events and quick links. Home and approvals explains each block.

    Search

    The search box at the top of every page looks across people, policies, announcements and, where you have access, assets, documents, work orders, customers and suppliers.

    1. Click the box that says Search people, policies, assets… and type at least two characters.
    2. Press Enter. Results appear grouped by kind: People, Policies, Announcements, Assets, Documents, Work orders, Customers, Suppliers.
    3. Click a result to open it.

    Search shows you only what you may open. If “Nothing found” appears for something you know exists, it may be at a site you have no access to, or in a feature that is hidden from your role. On Home there is a separate box, Find a colleague, that goes straight to the people directory.

    The notifications bell

    The bell in the top bar shows how many unread notifications you have. Click it to open Notifications. See Notifications and reminders.

    Approvals

    When something needs your sign-off (an expense report, a purchase request, a document, a change), it appears under My day → Approvals and on Home. See Home and approvals.

    Saving and autosaved drafts

    You rarely need to worry about losing a half-finished form. About one second after you stop typing, the system saves what you entered on the form as a draft, on the server, under your name.

    • If you leave the page, close the tab or lose power, open the same page again, on any device you sign in to. Your entries are put back, and a line at the top of the form says “Your unsaved entries from [time] are back.” with a Discard them button.
    • Drafts you have not finished are listed on Home under Unsaved entries, with the time you last touched them. Click one to return to it.
    • A draft disappears when the form is saved successfully, or when you press the form's Cancel button, or when you choose Discard them.
    • Passwords, file attachments and hidden fields are never kept. Some editors with growing lists keep their own draft instead.

    Drafts save what you typed, not the record. Nothing is submitted, approved or visible to others until you press the form's own save or submit button.

    Sorting and filtering tables

    Most lists are tables. You can reshape them without changing any data:

    1. Sort: click a column heading. Click again to reverse, and a third time to clear the sort. Blank values always sort to the end.
    2. Search the rows: use the small search box above the table. Each word you type must appear somewhere in the row.
    3. Filter by column: choose Column filters, then type in the box under a column heading to keep only rows whose cell contains that text.
    4. Start again: choose Reset to clear the search, the filters and the sort.

    Sorting and filtering apply to the rows already shown. Some pages fetch only part of a long list; those pages have their own filters (such as a site, a status or a date range) above the table. Use those first.

    Signing out safely

    Choose Sign out at the top right. For single sign-on, the platform also ends its session on the server.

    If you made changes without a connection (hours, parts used, or a status change on a work order) and they have not been sent yet, the system warns you first: “N changes made offline have not been sent yet and will be lost. Sign out anyway?” Choose Cancel, get back online, wait for the bar at the top to clear (or use Send now), and then sign out. Signing out also clears the saved copies kept on that device. See Working offline and on tablets.

    Your sign-in belongs to one browser tab. Closing the tab ends your session on that device, so on a shared computer close it when you are done.
