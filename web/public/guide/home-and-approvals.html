    My day

    Home and approvals

    Read your Home page, deal with what waits for your sign-off, delegate while you are away, and confirm periodic reviews.

    On this page
    • Your Home page
    • Approvals
    • Reviews
    • Seeing what is late
    • Common mistakes

    Home is where each day starts: the company's news, a short list of what needs you, and quick links. Approvals is where you sign off requests that came to you. This page covers both, plus Reviews (records that must be looked at again every so often) and what to do when you are away.

    Your Home page

    Home greets you by first name and shows the date. From top to bottom:

    Find a colleague
    A search box at the top. Type a name, team or site and press Enter to open the people directory with the search applied.
    Company news
    The latest four announcements. Safety notices, events, people news and IT news have their own labels. All news and events opens the full list. Hidden if your company has switched news off for your role.
    My work
    A short list of counts, each a link: Waiting for my sign-off, Policies to read and acknowledge, My actions, My open work orders, My training to do and Overdue work orders at my sites. A coloured count means something is overdue or waiting. Items only appear if you have the matching feature and role.
    Documents to read
    Controlled documents you must read and acknowledge, with the due date or Overdue. Open each one and acknowledge it on its own page.
    Unsaved entries
    Forms you started and did not save; they are kept for you on any device. Click one to continue. See Saving and autosaved drafts.
    Coming up
    Events with a date, from the news page.
    Quick links
    The employee handbook, the people directory, and links your company added. Links to other websites open in a new tab.

    Below those blocks, tables list My work orders, Waiting for my sign-off and My training, as far as they apply to you. Click a row to open it.

    Administrators may also see a card called Worth a look with a suggestion for something to set up. Choose Show me, Not now, or Stop these suggestions. At most one is shown a week; see How I like to work.

    Approvals

    An approval is a request that needs one or more people to say yes. Approvals appear under My day → Approvals and on Home. The page title is Waiting for my sign-off.

    The list contains only steps you can sign right now: earlier stages are finished, you hold the role the step is addressed to (or the step is addressed to you personally, or you are covering for someone), and you are not the person who raised the request.

    What can arrive

    Label on the cardWhat you are approving
    Change review, Startup authorizationA change request or its start-up review. See Changes and notices.
    Purchase requestA request to buy. The card shows the total, whether it is capital spend, who asked, and any reason it needed approval. See Purchasing.
    Quote pricingA quote priced below the company's limits. See Quotes and orders.
    Document review, Document signatureA new document version. A signature asks you to enter your sign-in credential again. See Document control.
    FMEA review, Continuity plan approvalRisk analyses and continuity plans. See Quality and Continuity.
    Expense reportsThese appear here too, and you can also decide them on the report itself. See Expenses.

    Deciding on an approval

    1. Open Approvals. Each request is a card. The first line says what it is and which step is yours (for example Finance review). If you are covering for someone, it says on behalf of their name.
    2. Click the title to open the full record and check the details. Some cards link a Justification presentation.
    3. Type a comment. It is required to reject, and for some reviews it is required to approve too; then the label reads “Result of your review (required: what you checked and any conditions)”.
    4. If the card asks you to sign, enter your sign-in credential in the box provided (at least 8 characters).
    5. Choose Approve as [your role] or Reject. The card disappears and the requester is notified.

    Anyone who holds the role a step is addressed to, at the request's site, can approve it, or a person named personally on the step, or a delegate (below). You can never approve your own request. Separation of duties is deliberate. A very small company may allow the requester to approve their own expense report or bill if nobody else could; the record shows it as self-approved.

    What the statuses mean

    StatusMeaning
    PendingThe step is open and waiting for a decision.
    ApprovedThe step was signed. When all stages are approved, the request goes ahead.
    RejectedA reviewer said no. The requester sees your comment and normally revises and resubmits.
    CancelledThe requester withdrew the request.
    SupersededThe request was changed and replaced by a newer revision, so earlier approvals no longer count.

    Away? Delegate your sign-offs

    At the bottom of the Approvals page, Away? Delegate your sign-offs lets someone else sign for you for a set time. Their signature records that it was on your behalf.

    1. Under Delegate to, choose a person.
    2. Set From and Until dates.
    3. Give a Reason, such as “vacation”.
    4. Choose Delegate. The table shows Scheduled before the start date, Active during, and Ended after you press End.

    Delegating does not give the person your permissions. It only lets them sign the steps addressed to you. A delegate cannot approve your own requests.

    Reviews

    My day → Reviews appears for people who manage records that must be looked at again every so often: FMEAs, procedures, policies, course content, suppliers, PM plans, spares lists, control limits, user access and more. It lists what is overdue or due soon.

    1. Choose the kind of record, or leave All kinds. Choose Due soon or overdue or All, with their next date.
    2. On a row, choose Record review. Choose Still current, no change if the record is still correct, or Changes needed (revise it through its own approval) and say what needs to change (required).
    3. Choose Save review. The next review counts from today. If changes are needed, make them on the record itself; they go through that record's normal approval.
    4. If you manage the record, you can also set a frequency of its own in months with Set its own frequency.

    If a review stays overdue, the people above you are reminded. How often each kind of record is reviewed is set by administrators.

    Review frequency and who is reminded are set under Admin → Reminders. See Administrator brief: reminders and retention.

    Seeing what is late

    For everything that is overdue across your work, use What is late in the Insight section.

    Common mistakes

    • Waiting for a request that never arrives. If you expected a step and do not see it, an earlier stage may still be open, the step may be addressed to someone else, or you raised it yourself.
    • Rejecting without a comment. The Reject button stays grey until you type one.
    • Forgetting to end a delegation. It ends on its date, but if you return early, choose End.
