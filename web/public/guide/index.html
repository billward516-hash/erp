    Welcome

    What the platform is, where each part of this guide lives, and where to start for your job.

    On this page
    • What the system is
    • Where things are in this guide
    • Where do I start, by role?
    • How to read this guide

    This guide explains how to use your company's operations platform: the one place for work orders, purchasing, quality, safety, people matters, books and reports. It is written for the people who use it every day, not for programmers. Where something is only for the person who sets the system up, you will see a short box that points to the Administrator brief.

    What the system is

    Think of it as your company's intranet with the work built in. When you sign in you land on Home: company news, the things waiting for you, and quick links. The menu on the left leads to the areas of the business you are allowed to use. Each area keeps its own records (a work order, a purchase order, a nonconformance, an expense report), and records that need a decision go to the right person as an approval.

    A few ideas run through everything:

    • You see what your job needs. The menu, the pages and the buttons depend on your role and on the sites you work at. See Roles and access.
    • Nothing is lost half-way. Forms keep what you typed while you work, and the technician screens keep working without a connection. See Getting started and Working offline and on tablets.
    • Decisions leave a trail. Approvals, sign-offs and changes are recorded with who did them and when.
    • The system tells you what is late. Notifications, reminders and the What is late page pull overdue work into one place.

    How to read this guide

    • Names in a box like Save are exactly what you see on screen: buttons, tabs and menu entries.
    • Numbered steps describe a task from start to finish.
    • Who can do this boxes name the role that holds the right. If you do not have it, the button or page will not appear, or the system will say you may not.
    • Administrator boxes are short notes for the person who configures the system, with a link to the full steps.
    • Use the search box at the top of the guide to find a word, such as “timesheet” or “supplier”. The print view gives you a clean copy of any page.

    Your menu may not show every area described in this guide. That is normal: your company chooses which parts to switch on, and your role decides the rest. Why some menu items are missing explains how to tell which reason applies.
