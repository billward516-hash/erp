    Sales and supply

    Leasing

    Track who rents each unit, bill monthly rent, handle notices and move-outs, and get empty units ready again.

    On this page
    • The three tabs
    • Rent roll
    • Create a lease
    • Renew, give notice, move out
    • Unit turns
    • Bill the rent
    • What is late
    • Common mistakes

    Leasing is for companies that rent out property. It shows every rental unit, who lives or works in it, what rent they pay, what they owe, and how long empty units stay empty. Open Sales & supply → Leasing.

    Leasing is part of the Standard edition. The Lite edition does not include it.

    Who can do this:

    • See the rent roll and unit turns: property managers, finance approvers, billing clerks and executives (permission "view leasing").
    • Create and change leases, record notices and move-outs, add make-ready jobs, mark units ready, bill rent: property managers (permission "manage leasing"), for their own properties.

    The three tabs

    Pick a property in the box at the top right, or leave it on All my properties. Tabs: Rent roll, Unit turns and, if you can manage leasing, Rent billing.

    Rent roll

    The rent roll has one row per unit. The tiles above it show:

    • Occupancy: occupied units (including those with notice given) out of rentable units. Units set offline are not counted.
    • Rent + charges a month: monthly rent and recurring charges on occupied units.
    • Vacant at market rent: what the empty units would bring in at their market rent.
    • Past due: rent unpaid after the grace days.
    Unit statusMeaning
    OccupiedA lease is in force.
    NoticeThe renter has given notice. The move-out date is shown.
    VacantEmpty. The number of days vacant is shown, and any make-ready target date.
    OfflineNot available to rent.

    Rows with money past due have a red edge. Click a row with a lease to open it. If there are no units, ask your administrator to import the unit directory, then rental units and leases (see Importing data).

    Create a lease

    1. On a vacant unit with no lease waiting, click New lease.
    2. Enter the renter's name (required), email and phone.
    3. Set From and, unless the lease is month to month, To. Leave To empty for month to month.
    4. Check the monthly rent (it starts at the unit's market rent) and the deposit. Add one other monthly charge, such as parking, with an amount.
    5. Click Create draft lease. The lease opens as a draft.
    6. When the lease is signed, click Signed: start the lease. Only then does the lease take the unit.

    The renter becomes a customer in your customer list, so their invoices, payments and reminders live in Receivables.

    Lease statusMeaning
    DraftCreated, not yet signed. A draft can be cancelled.
    ActiveIn force. The unit is occupied.
    NoticeThe renter has given notice to vacate.
    EndedMoved out. The unit goes into a turn.

    Renew, give notice, move out

    Open a lease. The Next step block shows the actions that fit its status.

    1. Renew: click Renew, set the New end date (empty for month to month) and the new rent, and click Renew. The new lease starts the day after the old one ends, with no turn in between.
    2. Notice to vacate: click Notice to vacate, enter the date notice was given and the date they are moving out, and click Record notice. The unit shows Notice in the rent roll.
    3. Move-out: click Record move-out, set the date they moved out (not in the future) and how many days you give yourself to make the unit ready (Make ready within, 7 by default), and click Record move-out. The unit becomes vacant and a unit turn opens.

    Unit turns

    A turn is the make-ready period between one renter and the next. It opens when a move-out is recorded. The Unit turns tab shows the property and unit, when the renter moved out, the target ready date (with a red late badge if missed), days so far, jobs done out of jobs, Rent lost so far (market rent per day) and any lease already lined up.

    1. Click + job on a turn, describe the work (paint, clean, carpet, replace blinds) and click Add work order. Each job is a work order on the unit, which you can send to a contractor from the work order. See Work orders.
    2. When every job is done, click Mark ready. The button is disabled while jobs are still open.

    Tick Open turns only to hide finished turns.

    Bill the rent

    1. Open the Rent billing tab. The month defaults to the current month (or next month after the 20th).
    2. Read the preview: one row for each lease not yet billed for that month, with its lines and total. A prorated note marks an amount that was prorated.
    3. Click Issue N rent invoices to issue them, or Create as drafts to review them first in Receivables.

    Rent invoices are dated the 1st and due on receipt. Late means unpaid after the lease's grace days. Running the same month again only bills leases not yet billed. Payments, statements and reminders are handled in Receivables. The deposit is recorded as agreed; the accounting system holds the money.

    What is late

    Leases about to end and unit turns running late are in the daily reminders. The Rent roll and occupancy report in Reports gives the same data for export.

    Common mistakes

    • Forgetting to click Signed: start the lease. A draft lease does not occupy the unit and is not billed.
    • Recording a move-out without a make-ready target. The default of 7 days sets the late date; change it if the unit needs longer.
    • Billing the wrong month. Check the Month field before you issue.

    Properties are sites of kind "property"; units, leases and renters are normally loaded with Data import. Rent invoices post through Receivables to your accounting connection or the built-in books. See Sites and people, Importing data and Accounting connection.
