    Company

    News, people and policies

    Read company news and events, find colleagues, read and acknowledge the handbook and policies, and see the expense rules; plus how HR publishes them.

    On this page
    • News and events
    • People
    • Policies and the handbook
    • Common mistakes

    The Company section of the menu is the everyday intranet: News and events, People and Policies & handbook. Everyone can read them. A few roles can write them. (Improvements has its own page.)

    News and events

    This is the company noticeboard. Each item has a kind, which sets its label: News, Event, Safety, People or IT. An item is either for everyone or for one site, and may have an event date, which makes it appear under Coming up on Home. Pinned items stay at the top. The latest four items also show on Home.

    Reading

    Open Company → News and events for the full list. Each item shows its kind, date, author and site (or “everyone”), and the date of the event if it has one. Bodies can include headings, lists and bold text.

    Posting an announcement

    People who post announcements: HR managers and plant managers in the starter roles, and administrators.

    1. Open News and events. The form Post an announcement is at the top.
    2. Type a Headline and the Message. Use ## Heading for a heading, - item for a list and **bold** for bold.
    3. Choose the kind, and who it is for: Everyone or one site.
    4. Optionally set an Event date, a Show until date, and Pin to the top.
    5. Tick Also send a notification if people should be pinged. This is ticked and fixed on for Safety items, so safety notices always notify.
    6. Choose Post. To take one down, choose Withdraw next to it; it stays in the list for posters, marked Withdrawn.

    Posters also keep the Quick links on the home page at the bottom: add a title and an address (a full web address, or a path inside the platform that starts with /), or Remove a link.

    People

    Company → People is the company directory: everyone, with work contact details only: name, job title, department, site and office, work email and work phone.

    1. Type a name, job, team or site in the box and choose Search (or use Find a colleague on Home).
    2. The count above the cards says how many people match.
    3. Click an email address to start a message, or a phone number to call from a phone.

    The directory shows only what a work directory needs. Private HR details are not included and stay with people who manage employee records. To correct your own entry, ask HR.

    Policies and the handbook

    Company → Policies & handbook holds the rules the company works by. It has three tabs.

    Employee handbook
    The handbook sections in order, with a contents list. Sections you have not yet acknowledged, because they are new or changed, carry a New tag.
    All policies
    Every policy that applies to you, with its number, title, kind (Handbook, Expenses, HR, IT, Safety, Quality, Finance, Other), the site it applies to, the revision in force and your status.
    Expense rules
    The current limits and receipt rules. See below.

    Reading and acknowledging

    Some policies require you to confirm that you have read them. When one is published or changed in a way that needs a fresh confirmation, you get a notification, and it shows under Policies to read and acknowledge on Home.

    1. Open Policies & handbook → All policies and look for To acknowledge in the You column, or open the handbook.
    2. Read the policy. For the handbook, a banner says how many sections are new or changed since you last acknowledged.
    3. Choose I have read this policy (on a policy) or I have read the handbook (in the handbook). The tag changes to Acknowledged with the date.
    You seeMeaning
    To acknowledgeYou must read and confirm.
    AcknowledgedYou have confirmed the version in force.
    For referencePublished for information; no confirmation needed.
    Not publishedStill a draft, visible only to people who write policies.

    A policy that is only slightly reworded (a “minor change”) keeps earlier acknowledgements. A substantive change asks everyone to confirm again.

    Expense rules

    The Expense rules tab shows what expense reports are checked against: the amount above which an itemized receipt is required, the mileage rate per mile (it says “a rate the company has not set yet” until one exists), the number of days within which expenses should be submitted, and, per category, the limit per expense, the limit per day and the receipt threshold, plus which categories also need to know who attended and why. The same table appears inside the expense and travel policy, so there is one source of truth.

    Writing and publishing policies

    Writing policies and the handbook, setting the expense rules, and seeing who has acknowledged is for HR managers, payroll administrators and administrators (the “manage policies” right). Everyone reads published policies.

    1. On All policies, under Add a policy, type a title, choose the kind and choose Add. Or choose Add missing template sections (or Start from templates on an empty handbook) to create draft handbook sections and an expense and travel policy. Templates are not legal advice; have them reviewed.
    2. Open the policy. Choose Edit draft (or Start a new revision for a published one) and write the text. Text in [[double brackets]] marks what your company still has to fill in.
    3. For a new revision, say what changed, and tick Minor change (wording only): keep earlier acknowledgements if it does not need re-confirming. Choose Save draft.
    4. When no placeholders are left, the page says Ready to publish. Pick an Effective date if you want, and choose Publish. Publish stays off while any placeholder remains.
    5. Track Acknowledgements on the policy: how many have confirmed and who is outstanding. History shows every revision, who published it and what changed.

    On the Expense rules tab, writers can edit the receipt threshold, the mileage rate and the submit-within days, and each category's limits.

    A policy can apply to everyone or to one site. Hiding Policies and handbook, News and events or People directory from a role is done under Admin → Features. See Administrator brief: features and editions.

    Common mistakes

    • Expecting ordinary news to ping people. Only safety items, and items where Also send a notification is ticked, reach the bell. Everything else waits on Home.
    • Publishing with placeholders. The system will not let you; fill in every [[bracket]] first.
    • Posting for one site by accident. Check For says Everyone when you mean everyone.
    • Not acknowledging. Reminders and the policy's outstanding list will keep naming you.
