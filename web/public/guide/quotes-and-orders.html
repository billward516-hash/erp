    Sales and supply

    Quotes and orders

    Build a quote, get pricing approved, send it, record the customer's acceptance, then ship and invoice the order.

    On this page
    • The sales catalog
    • Build a quote
    • Finish, approve and send
    • Validity and expiry
    • Record the customer's acceptance
    • Revise a quote
    • The sales order
    • Common mistakes

    A quote is your written price to a customer. When the customer accepts it, you record that acceptance and the system turns the quote into a sales order. You then ship the order and invoice what shipped. This page walks through each step and what the statuses mean.

    Quotes, orders and shipments are part of the Standard edition. The Lite edition has customers and invoices but no quotes or orders.

    • See quotes and orders: sales representatives and managers, plant managers, quality managers, finance approvers, billing clerks, bookkeepers, executives (permission "view sales").
    • Build, finish, send, revise, extend and record acceptance of quotes: sales representatives, sales managers and service dispatchers (permission "quote"). This is checked for the selling site.
    • Add catalog items, set a promise date, ship, make: sales managers and plant or operations managers (permission "manage orders").
    • Approve out-of-limit pricing: the sales manager role, from the Approvals page.
    • Release a credit hold: finance approvers (permission "release credit holds").

    The sales catalog

    Open Sales & supply → Quotes & orders and click the Catalog tab. It lists the products and services you sell with their list price and standard cost. If you can manage orders, add one with the form under the table: code, description, Product or Service, list price, standard cost and, for a stocked product, the part it ships from. Quote lines can pick catalog items, which brings in the list price and cost so the system can check discount and margin.

    Build a quote

    1. Open the customer (Customers) and click New quote. You can also arrive here from a customer request (Customer requests for quote).
    2. Choose the Customer, enter a Title (at least three letters) and pick a Contact and, if one applies, an Opportunity.
    3. Choose the Selling site. Only sites where you may quote are offered.
    4. Set Valid until. If you leave it empty the quote is valid for 30 days from today.
    5. Under Lines, choose a catalog item or Not in the catalog (describe it), then enter quantity and unit price. Leave the price empty to use the list price. A badge shows how far below list a line is. Click Add a line for more.
    6. Under Terms shown to the customer, fill in Lead time, Delivery, Included and Not included. Add Notes shown on the quote if needed.
    7. Click Save draft. Use Preview PDF on the next page to see the letter.

    The quote prints as a one-page letter with a validity banner, your terms, a signature block and page footers. Prices are stated excluding taxes. Tax and freight are not quote lines; they are added on the order's invoice.

    You can edit lines and terms while the quote is a draft (Edit lines, Edit terms shown to the customer). Once it has been finished, change it by making a new revision.

    Finish, approve and send

    1. On the quote, click Finish quote. The system checks the pricing.
    2. If every line is within 15% of list price and the margin on costed lines is at least 20%, the quote is ready to send. Otherwise a banner explains why, and the quote goes to a sales manager for Pricing approval. The manager decides from the Approvals page (Home and approvals).
    3. When the status is Ready to send, type the recipient in Send to (it defaults to the contact's email) and click Mark as sent (stores the PDF).

    The system does not email the quote. Mark as sent freezes that revision, stores the exact PDF and logs the send on the customer. Open the PDF from the Sent table and send it yourself. The stored PDF is never changed afterward.

    Quote statuses

    StatusMeaningWhat you can do
    DraftBeing written.Edit lines and terms, then finish it.
    Pricing approvalOutside the discount or margin limits; waiting for a sales manager.Wait, or start a new revision.
    Ready to sendPricing is cleared.Mark as sent, extend validity, or revise.
    SentThe customer has the PDF.Record acceptance or a decline, extend validity, or revise.
    AcceptedThe customer said yes; an order exists.Open the order.
    DeclinedThe customer said no; the reason is kept.Start a new revision if they come back.
    CancelledNo longer going ahead.Nothing.

    Validity and expiry

    A red Expired badge appears next to a ready or sent quote whose Valid until date has passed. Expiry is worked out from the date; it is not a separate status. An expired quote cannot be accepted. Extend it or revise it first.

    On the quote list, the Validity filter shows Expiring within 7 days or Expired, and Time left counts the days. The quote owner is reminded 7 days before, 2 days before, and the day after a sent quote's expiry. Sent quotes that expired with no decision also appear on What is late.

    Extend a quote's validity

    1. On a quote that is Ready to send or Sent, set Extend validity to a date later than the current one.
    2. Type a Reason, such as "customer's budget approval pending".
    3. Click Extend.

    The old date, new date, reason, who and when are kept in Validity history, together with the original date. The PDF you already sent does not change; the customer's copy still shows the old date, so tell them in writing.

    Record the customer's acceptance

    1. When the customer says yes, open the sent quote. Under Record the customer's acceptance enter Accepted by (name) and Their title.
    2. Choose How they accepted: Purchase order, Signed quote, Email, Verbal or Customer portal.
    3. Set Date accepted. It cannot be in the future or before the quote was sent.
    4. Enter the Customer PO number. It is required when the method is Purchase order.
    5. Add a Note if they said something worth keeping, such as "ship in two lots".
    6. Click Customer accepted: create order. Then scan or upload the signed quote or PO under Files on the same page.

    The system records who, how, when, the revision and the total that was accepted, and creates the sales order from that revision. The opportunity moves to Won and the customer record becomes a customer. If the customer says no, type a reason in the box and click Customer declined.

    Revise a quote

    Type what changes in the Reason box and click New revision. The quote goes back to Draft with the next revision number and any earlier pricing approval no longer counts. Each revision you sent stays in the Sent table.

    The sales order

    Open orders from the Orders tab. The table shows the customer, their PO, the total, the promised date and the status.

    Order statusMeaning
    OpenNothing shipped yet.
    Partly shippedSome quantity has gone out.
    ShippedEverything has shipped.
    CancelledNot going ahead.
    Late (red badge)The promised ship date has passed and the order is still open or partly shipped.

    Set the promise date

    On an open order, enter a date next to Promise ship date and click Set.

    Credit hold

    If accepting the quote puts the customer over their credit limit (open invoices plus uninvoiced orders), the order is created on credit hold. A red box explains why and nothing can ship. Finance is notified; a finance approver types why it can ship (payment received, limit raised, prepaid) and clicks Release hold.

    Make what is not in stock

    If you can manage production and a line is for a product that can be made, the Production column shows Make with the quantity. Clicking it opens a production order for the open quantity. See Production orders.

    Ship

    1. On the order, find the Ship block. For each line, set Shipping now (it starts at what is open, limited by stock on hand) and, for stocked items, the storeroom in From. Lot- or serial-tracked parts ask you to pick a lot.
    2. Enter the Carrier and Tracking number if you have them.
    3. Click Record shipment.

    Stocked items leave the storeroom when you record the shipment. A line set to 0 stays open for later. Shipments are listed under Shipments.

    Invoice what shipped

    Click Invoice what has shipped in the Invoices block, or use the Ready to invoice tab in Receivables. A draft invoice opens for review. See Receivables.

    Common mistakes

    • Waiting too long to record acceptance. The acceptance date cannot be before the send date, and an expired quote must be extended first.
    • Extending without telling the customer. Their PDF still shows the old date.
    • Entering a price instead of leaving it blank. A typed price below 85% of list triggers approval; leaving it blank uses list.

    The approval limits (15% below list, 20% margin) are built in and the pricing approval goes to the sales manager role. Make sure someone holds that role at each selling site. Reminder timing for expiring quotes is under Reminders. See Roles and access, Reminders and retention and Features and editions.
