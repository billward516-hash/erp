    Finance

    Receivables

    Invoice customers, record what they pay, issue credits and chase what is overdue.

    On this page
    • The page
    • Invoice what has shipped (Standard)
    • Type an invoice (no sales order)
    • Issue the invoice
    • Record a payment
    • Chase overdue accounts
    • Credit memos
    • Customer account
    • Lite and Standard
    • Common mistakes

    Receivables is the money customers owe you. Open Finance → Receivables (the page heading reads Customer invoices). Here you create and issue invoices, record payments, work the list of overdue customers and issue credit memos.

    In the Standard edition, invoices usually come from shipped sales orders. In Lite you type simple invoices directly. In both, issued invoices go to your accounting system or post to the built-in books, depending on your setup.

    • See invoices, collections and customer accounts: billing clerks, finance approvers, bookkeepers, sales managers, property managers, executives (permission "view receivables").
    • Create, issue and void invoices, record payments, log collection calls, send reminders, issue credit memos: billing clerks, finance approvers, bookkeepers, property managers (permission "manage receivables").
    • Release an order from credit hold: finance approvers (permission "release credit holds").

    The page

    Three tiles show Open receivables (balance on issued invoices), Overdue and Drafts. Tabs below them: Invoices, New invoice, Ready to invoice, Collections, Record payment and Credit memos. New invoice and Record payment only show if you can manage receivables. Ready to invoice is for Standard only.

    StatusMeaning
    DraftBeing prepared. No number yet. Not sent anywhere.
    IssuedNumbered, dated and sent on to accounting or the books. Nothing paid yet.
    Part paidSome payment has been applied.
    PaidFully paid.
    VoidCancelled, with a reason.
    Overdue (red badge)Issued or part paid and past its due date.

    Invoice what has shipped (Standard)

    1. Click the Ready to invoice tab. It lists orders with shipped quantity that has not been invoiced yet.
    2. Click Create invoice on an order. A draft invoice opens with the shipped lines. (You can also click Invoice what has shipped on the order itself.)
    3. Review the draft. See Issue the invoice below.

    Only one draft per order can exist at a time. To invoice part of what shipped now and the rest later, set a line's quantity to 0 on the draft.

    Type an invoice (no sales order)

    1. Click New invoice.
    2. Choose the Customer. Add Their PO (optional).
    3. For each line enter a description, quantity, price and income type: Services, Products, Rent or Freight. Click Add line for more.
    4. Enter Sales tax $ if it applies. The total updates as you type.
    5. Click Create draft.

    Issue the invoice

    1. Open the draft. Click Preview PDF to see what the customer will get.
    2. To change it, click Edit draft. You can change freight, sales tax, the note on the invoice and each line's quantity. Save with Save draft.
    3. Click Issue invoice with the total on it. Issuing assigns the number, fixes the due date from the customer's terms, stores the PDF and sends the invoice to accounting on the next sync.

    After issuing you cannot edit the invoice. To correct it, void it (with a reason) and start again, or issue a credit memo.

    To throw away a draft, type a reason and click Discard draft. For an issued invoice the button reads Void invoice.

    Record a payment

    If your company's accounting system supplies payments, they arrive on each sync and you will see a note saying payments are not typed in. Otherwise:

    1. Click Record payment and choose the customer. Only customers with open invoices are listed.
    2. Enter Received on, the Amount, the Method (check, ACH, wire, card or other) and a check number or reference.
    3. The amount spreads over the customer's open invoices, oldest due date first. Change the Apply amounts to direct it elsewhere.
    4. Check the line Applied … of …. The two numbers must match.
    5. Click Record payment.

    You can also record a payment from an invoice or a customer's account page.

    Chase overdue accounts

    The Collections tab lists customers with overdue invoices, longest overdue first. It shows the contact and phone, the amount overdue, total open, how many days the oldest is overdue, the time of the last contact (a None badge if never) and any promise to pay. A promise date turns red when it passes. Accounts with an invoice more than 60 days overdue have a red edge.

    1. Call the customer.
    2. Click Log call on their row, write What was said, and set Promised to pay by (optional).
    3. Click Save.

    On an open invoice you can also log a call against that invoice, or click Email payment reminder. The reminder goes to the customer's primary contact and is sent with the next mail run. You can send only one reminder per invoice per day. If the customer has no primary contact email, the button is disabled and says so. Overdue invoices also appear on What is late and trigger a weekly reminder summary to receivables staff at the site.

    Credit memos

    A credit memo reduces what a customer owes: a return, a price correction or goodwill. It is available only where payments are typed in (not when an accounting system issues credits).

    1. Open the issued or paid invoice and expand Credit this invoice. Or open the customer's account and use New credit memo.
    2. Enter the quantity to credit on each line. If goods come back, choose the returned part and the storeroom it goes into (not available in Lite); for lot- or serial-tracked parts, enter the lot or serial numbers.
    3. Add Sales tax credited and a Reason that shows on the memo.
    4. Click Draft credit memo, review it, then click Issue credit memo. Issuing numbers it, stores the PDF, returns goods to stock and applies it to the invoice it was raised against.

    Any credit left over is shown as Left to use. Use it from the memo page either to Apply it to another open invoice or to Record refund by check, ACH, wire, card or other. A memo that has been neither applied nor refunded can be voided with a reason.

    Customer account

    A customer's account page (open it from the invoice or the collections list) shows open invoices, ordered-but-not-invoiced orders, the Credit limit and Exposure. If exposure is over the limit, it is flagged and new orders from that customer go on credit hold. See Quotes and orders.

    Lite and Standard

    Lite has New invoice, payments, collections and credit memos, with issued invoices and payments posting to the built-in books. There is no Ready to invoice tab because there are no orders. Rent invoices from Leasing also land here (Leasing).

    Common mistakes

    • Issuing before checking the PDF. An issued invoice is final.
    • Applying a payment to the wrong invoice. Check the Apply column before you record.
    • Not logging calls. The collections list uses the last contact date to show who has not been chased.

    Whether payments are typed in or come from accounting depends on the accounting connection. Payment reminders are emailed by the mail adapter. See Accounting connection, Books and Reminders and retention.
