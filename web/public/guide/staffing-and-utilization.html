    Staffing and utilization

    See who is booked on which project, how much capacity is left, and how much of the available time is billed.

    On this page
    • The capacity grid
    • Assign people to projects
    • Utilization
    • Time off
    • Setup
    • Reports
    • Reminders
    • Common mistakes

    Service companies → Staffing answers three questions: who is planned on which project, who has room (or too much), and how much of people's available time turns into billable work. It uses working hours, holidays, time off, the assignments you make, and approved timesheets.

    Everyone with an employee record sees their own capacity and utilization, and the people who report to them (through the manager set on the employee record). Operations managers, HR managers, executives and district managers can see the people at their sites. Operations managers and HR managers (and administrators) can change assignments, working patterns, holidays, targets and decide time off, at the sites where they hold the permission. Company-wide settings need the permission at all sites. The feature can be switched off or limited to roles under Admin → Features (Standard edition).

    The capacity grid

    The Capacity tab shows people down the side and weeks across. Each cell reads booked / available hours and says its state in words:

    StateMeaning
    Over-allocatedBooked hours are above available hours (by more than the tolerance set under Setup, half an hour unless changed).
    Fully bookedBooked hours use up the available hours.
    Part bookedSome hours are booked and some are free.
    UnbookedThe person has hours available and nothing booked.
    Not workingNo available hours that week and nothing booked (for example a week of leave).

    Colour reinforces the words; it is never the only signal. The grid works from the keyboard: tab to a person's name or a cell and press Enter to open that person below the grid. Use the arrows to move the weeks, and the menus to narrow by site, department or manager.

    How available hours are worked out

    For each day: the person's working pattern for that weekday (or the company's default week when they have none), less any company holiday for their site, less approved time off. Field service technician time off counts as full days off. A person's own pattern starts on its date and stays until a later one starts.

    Assign people to projects

    1. Open a person from the grid and choose Add an assignment.
    2. Pick the project, give the hours a week, the dates and whether it is Tentative or Confirmed. Add a role and a note if useful. Whether the time is billable starts from the project: a non-billable project defaults to non-billable.
    3. Choose Check and add. If the booking would push any week over capacity, the page lists those weeks and still lets you Save anyway. The reason you type is kept with the assignment (shown as the conflict note).

    Hours a week are spread over the days the person works. A booking that starts on Wednesday counts the share of the week from Wednesday on. A company holiday shrinks the booking in proportion (a full-time booking in a holiday week is not a conflict). Personal time off does not shrink it, so a booked person who is away shows as over-allocated, which is the cue to move the work.

    Use Edit or move to change hours, dates, state, or to move the assignment to another person; Remove frees the hours. Every create, move, edit and removal is recorded; History shows who did what, with the conflict note. Projects at sites you do not manage cannot be used. Only projects that are not finished are offered.

    Utilization

    Utilization is billable hours divided by available hours, for a period you choose, grouped by person, department, manager, site or job title.

    • Billable hours come only from approved timesheets (including those already sent to payroll), on time entries marked billable. Hours on submitted timesheets that are not approved yet are shown in their own column and are not counted.
    • Only whole days that have passed count; the page says through which date.
    • A person with no approved timesheet in the period has no utilization (shown as No approved time), not 0%. Team figures include only people with approved time. When nobody has any, the page says so instead of showing zeros.
    • Targets: a person's own target wins, then the target for their job title (matched to the job title on the employee record), then the company target (75% until changed). Team targets are weighted by available hours.

    People who are not staffed on projects (an owner, office staff) can be left out with Leave this person out on their page. They disappear from utilization, the bench report, the forecast and the reminders.

    Time off

    On the Time off tab you can ask for time off (whole days, or some hours a day). A manager with the permission at your site approves or rejects it; nobody decides their own. Approved time off reduces available hours. When a manager enters time off for someone else it is recorded as approved straight away. Managers are told of new requests; you are told of the decision.

    Setup

    The Setup tab (managers only) holds the default working week, the company target, the bench threshold (people booked under this share of capacity are on the bench), the overbooking tolerance, company holidays (for every site, or one site; whole day or some hours) and utilization targets. Individual working patterns are set on each person's page with Set a pattern.

    Holidays are not preloaded; add the ones your company observes each year. Targets by job title match the job title text on the employee record, ignoring capitals.

    Reports

    Under Insight → Reports (CSV, Excel and PDF as for other reports):

    • Utilization: the table above, for the period and grouping you pick.
    • Bench and overbooking: people booked below the bench threshold over the next weeks (four by default), and each over-allocated week.
    • Staffing forecast: capacity against confirmed and tentative bookings for the next 13 weeks. It shows what is planned, not what will be sold.

    Reminders

    The daily job can tell staffing managers at each site about: people over-allocated next week, people with nothing booked in the next two weeks, and, on the last day of the month, people below their utilization target for the month. Each is a single summary per site and is not repeated within its repeat setting. Administrators can switch them off or change them under Admin → Reminders. See Notifications and reminders.

    Common mistakes

    • Utilization looks too low. Check for weeks whose timesheets are not approved yet; they are counted as available but their billable hours are not counted until approval.
    • Nobody on the bench list. People left out of staffing, and people with no available hours, are not listed.
    • A person is missing. They need an active employee record with a home site, and you need the permission at that site (or they report to you).

    People record billable time against projects on their timesheet; the project and billing pages of this section explain that side.
