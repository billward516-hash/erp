    Training and performance

    Take assigned training and assessments, see your qualifications, and take part in performance reviews, check-ins and goals.

    On this page
    • Training
    • Performance
    • Common mistakes

    This page covers two people topics in My day: Training, where you complete courses assigned to you and see which qualifications you hold, and Performance, where you and your manager hold reviews and check-ins and agree goals.

    Training

    My day → Training (title Training and qualifications) has up to three tabs. The first two need you to be linked to an employee record.

    My training
    Courses assigned to you, with a status and a due date. Overdue ones are flagged.
    My qualifications
    What you are authorized to do, with the date it took effect, when it expires, and a certificate to open as a PDF where one was issued. External credentials (licences, certificates from outside bodies) are listed beneath.
    Team records
    Everyone's training and results, for trainers and HR only.

    Anyone with training access (most roles) sees their own tabs. Team records needs the right to see other people's training records, held by trainers, HR managers and maintenance managers because scores and evidence are sensitive. Assigning training is for trainers, HR and maintenance managers; recording attendance and shadowing is for trainers (instructors); signing practical evaluations is for evaluators.

    Completing a course does not authorize you to do the work. A qualification is a separate decision granted by an authorized person. Work orders and machines can require a qualification and will refuse a person without a current one.

    Complete an assigned course

    A course is a series of numbered steps. Each step has a kind and is completed by a particular person.

    Kind of stepWho completes it
    Video, Self-studyYou. Choose I have completed this when you have watched or read it. A linked document opens with Open and its number.
    ClassroomA trainer records attendance (Record attendance).
    On-the-job shadowingA trainer signs it off (Sign off shadowing).
    Practical evaluationAn evaluator watches you and signs Competent or Not yet competent with a note about what they observed. You cannot sign your own.
    AssessmentYou, by taking the quiz.
    1. Open Training → My training and click the course.
    2. Work through the steps in order. Each card shows what is done, who recorded it and when.
    3. For a step you complete yourself, do the work and choose I have completed this. For steps that need a trainer or evaluator, the card tells you who records it (“A trainer records this for you.”).
    4. When every step is done, the course shows Training completed. If the course leads to a qualification, an authorized person still has to grant it.

    Taking an assessment

    1. On the assessment step, choose Take the assessment.
    2. Read the pass mark and which attempt this is (“attempt 1 of 3”, for example). Answer each question; where marked, select all answers that apply.
    3. Choose Submit answers. You see your score, and whether you passed.
    4. If you did not pass and have attempts left, take it again. If you have none left, ask your trainer to re-assign the course.

    Training statuses

    StatusMeaning
    AssignedGiven to you; nothing recorded yet.
    In progressAt least one step is done.
    CompletedAll steps are done.
    CancelledWithdrawn; you no longer need to complete it.
    OverdueA tag next to the due date: not finished by that date.

    Qualification statuses

    StatusMeaning
    ActiveCurrent. You may do the work it covers.
    ExpiredPast its expiry date. Renew before doing covered work.
    RevokedWithdrawn by an authorized person.
    Not yet effectiveGranted with a start date in the future.

    You are reminded when training is due and when a qualification is about to expire; see Notifications and reminders.

    Courses, assessments, qualification definitions and certificates are set up by people with course-authoring rights; periodic review of course content shows up under Reviews (see Home and approvals). Training can be hidden from roles under Admin → Features; if hidden, qualification checks on work orders and machines still apply. See Administrator brief: features and editions.

    Performance

    Performance holds reviews with your manager at the frequency your company sets, check-ins in between, and the goals you agree. Only you, your reviewer and HR can see yours. The menu entry Performance shows if you are linked to an employee record, or if you run the programme in HR.

    TabWho sees itWhat it shows
    My reviewsYouYour reviews and check-ins, and your open goals.
    My teamReviewersReviews and check-ins you are to complete for the people you review.
    EveryoneHREvery review, with counts of overdue, open and those without a reviewer.
    SettingsHRHow the programme runs.

    Your review

    A review opens automatically a few weeks before it is due, and you are notified. Your reviewer is normally your manager (set on your employee record), else your department head; HR assigns one if neither exists.

    1. Open Performance → My reviews and click your review. The headline tells you what is expected of you: for example “Your reviewer is preparing this. Add your self-assessment below.”
    2. If your company asks for one, write your Self-assessment: what went well, what was hard, and what you would like to work on. Choose Submit self-assessment. You can Update self-assessment while the review is open.
    3. Your reviewer writes the assessment and goals, then shares the review with you. You are notified: “Your performance review is ready to read and acknowledge”.
    4. Read it. Optionally add a comment and choose Acknowledge. Acknowledging says you have read it; it does not mean you agree with all of it.

    Reviewing someone

    1. Open My team and click a review.
    2. For a review, choose an Overall rating from your company's scale, and write Strengths, To develop and a Summary. For a check-in, write What was discussed.
    3. Add Goals with a measure and a due date. When a goal ends, set its outcome: met, partly met, not met or dropped.
    4. Choose Save draft as you go. When ready, choose Share with the employee (for a check-in, Complete check-in). A rating and a summary are required.

    Review statuses

    StatusMeaning
    OpenBeing prepared. The reviewer writes; the employee may add a self-assessment.
    Waiting for acknowledgementShared with the employee, who has yet to acknowledge it.
    CompletedAcknowledged (or closed by HR without acknowledgement), or a finished check-in.
    CancelledCancelled by HR with a reason, such as a leaver or long leave.
    Overdue (due date)Not completed by its due date.

    For HR

    Performance management is for the HR manager role (the right to run performance reviews).

    Under Settings choose how often to review (in months), whether everyone is reviewed in the same month or on each person's hire anniversary, the first review after hire, how often check-ins happen (0 for none), how many days before the due date a review opens, and whether employees are asked for a self-assessment. Saving the settings starts the programme; until then the page says reviews are not set up yet. On any open review, HR can Assign reviewer, Close without acknowledgement a shared review, or Cancel this review with a reason.

    Reminders to reviewers and to HR about overdue reviews are set under Admin → Reminders. Setting a manager on employee records is part of people setup.

    Common mistakes

    • Thinking a finished course means you are cleared for the work. Look under My qualifications.
    • Running out of assessment attempts. Ask your trainer; do not wait for the due date.
    • Leaving a review unshared. The employee sees nothing until the reviewer shares it.
    • Looking for Performance without an employee record. If your user is not linked to one, the entry is hidden.
